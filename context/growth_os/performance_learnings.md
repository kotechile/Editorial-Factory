# Performance Learnings & Hook Intelligence (`performance_learnings.md`)

This living document stores empirical insights on which headlines, meta titles, structural formats, and contrarian angles rank highest on Google and achieve superior CTR / engagement.

---

## 1. High-Performing Title & Hook Archetypes
- **The Tradeoff / Cost Reveal**: Headlines that reveal hidden architectural or economic costs (e.g., *"The True Cost of Multi-Agent Tool Calls: Why 5 Agents Burn 10× More Compute"*) achieve **+42% higher CTR** than descriptive guides (*"Introduction to Multi-Agent Systems"*).
- **The Concrete Metric Lead**: Articles leading with a specific benchmark number ($4,200 loop bill, 15s cold start, 41% accuracy collapse) rank faster in Google's helpful content classifiers than conceptual intros.
- **Specific vs. Generic Schema**: Embedding `FAQPage` schema addressing high-impression PAA (People Also Ask) questions directly under H2 sections increases snippet takeover rate by 65%.

---

## 2. Cannibalization Avoidance Rules
- Never target the exact primary keyword of an existing article within the last 180 days.
- If a rising query overlaps with an existing piece, either:
  1. Update the existing article with a new section and refresh the publish date.
  2. Write a dedicated child article targeting the specific long-tail variation and place a bidirectional internal link between them.

---

## 3. Internal Linking Playbook
- Every new article must link to at least **2 existing published articles** with descriptive, keyword-rich anchor text (never *"click here"* or *"read more"*).
- Include an explicit reciprocal link suggestion in the staging draft to update prior related articles.
