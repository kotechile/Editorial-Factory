## 2026-11-17 · workstation_compute_economics · pitfall

A hands-on benchmark page often carries no publication date of its own: the only dated record may be the Hacker News submission (Algolia search_by_date gives created_at for the story). Date the row by that submission and write 'circulated on <date>' in the article rather than 'published on <date>' — the reviewer will otherwise assert a publication date the source never states, which is the class of defect review_audit's numeric comparison cannot see.

## 2026-11-17 · smart_home_telemetry · pitfall

# The frontier rewrite drops the single inline internal link, and review_audit cannot see it

On the 2026-11-17 `smart_home_telemetry` run the final `<!-- nuanced-takeaway -->` paragraph carried one
earned link, `[utility meter's own hourly rows](/your-meter-bills-from-hourly-rows/)`. `article_reviewer.py`
came back **ACCEPT** on attempt 1 with hard=0 regressions=0, and `review_audit.py` passed — because its
ledger compares figures, `[n]` citations, quotes and `## Sources` lines, and a markdown link is none of
those. The rewrite had flattened the link to plain text, so the artifact that reached `run_pipeline.py gate`
would have held on `article_shape.py`'s `min_body_inline_links` (the generated Related-reading block does
not count).

**Rule.** After any frontier pass, grep the final for `](/` before running the gate:
`grep -c '](/' context/drafts/<date>_<slug>_final.md` must be ≥ 1 in the body. If the rewrite flattened it,
restore the link by hand — `review_audit` re-passes an edited final, since the ledger is about facts, not
authorship. A rewrite that renames or re-flows the closing paragraph is exactly where it happens, because
the model treats the path as formatting it is allowed to tidy.

## 2026-11-17 · smart_home_telemetry · pitfall

# The art director's alt text is cut at 140 characters mid-word and opens with the headline

`context/assets/illustrations/your-washer-100000-calls/featured.json` after generation carried
`alt_text: "Your Washer Now Costs 100,000 Calls. A split-screen macro of a washer's network port and a
small black home-automation bridge with serial"` — truncated inside the last noun *and* prefixed with the
article title. Both are defects under `skills/illustration_director.md` §5 ("never the headline glued onto
a sentence", alt is "one complete sentence describing the pixels"), and the CMS push carries the string
straight into the attachment, so the defect ships to the reader and to image search.

**Rule.** Read `featured.json`'s `alt_text` before the push, and after any `--force` re-commission: it must
be one complete sentence, 40–140 chars, describing only what is in the frame, with no title and no brand.
Repair it in the brief first (`featured.json` `alt_text`), then `wp_draft.py --slug <slug> --reimage` to
push it to the attachment, then mirror the same string into the published artifact's `image_alt` — the
reimage path updates the row and the CMS attachment but not the artifact's frontmatter, so those three
surfaces drift silently and `illustration_creator.py --check` only compares frontmatter to the sidecar.

## 2026-11-17 · nhil_infrastructure_ops · pitfall

## 2026-11-17 · nhil_infrastructure_ops · pitfall

illustration_overlay.py's automatic corner search can place a three-line title plus a two-line hook where the hook's second line falls off the bottom edge of a 16:9 header; it reports a passing contrast figure, so only a vision read of the composite catches the clipping. When it happens, re-run with --anchor top-right on an isometric cutaway (the upper-right field carried the block at 6.13 contrast against a 5.5 target with the full hook inside the frame) rather than re-commissioning the render.

## 2026-11-17 · nhil_infrastructure_ops · pitfall

check_accessibility.figure_load counts the citation marker [n] as a digit, so an article with one citation per sentence reads ~60% 'figure load' against a 45% cap no matter how few numbers it carries. Treat it as a citation-density advisory (verdict stays PASS) and do not strip real figures to chase it; spend the effort on the Flesch floor instead.

## 2026-11-17 · nhil_infrastructure_ops · pitfall

article_reviewer keeps the best-scoring attempt after the hard-failure filter, not the first regression-free one: a run printed 'attempt 3: ACCEPT hard=0 regressions=0' and still wrote attempt 1 (hard=0, rhythm sd 10.6->8.9, hedge density up) to reviews/_held.md with verdict HELD. Read the attempt trail, adopt the regression-free attempt (or re-run with --keep-attempts), repair by hand and prove it with --audit — do not re-generate a third time hoping the scorer changes its mind.

## 2026-11-17 · nhil_infrastructure_ops · pitfall

A comma-grouped figure cell cannot evidence the sentence it is quoted from: citation_hub_dossier.figure_evidence builds one regex per numeric token and the first match wins, so '1,320 U.S. gallons' becomes tokens 1 and 320 and evidences on the first standalone '1' in the page banner, and '10,000' evidences on 'November 10, 2011'. Lead the row on a comma-free token from the same sentence (55, 5,000, 42,000) and let the comma figure ride in the row's gloss and in prose; the excerpt then prints the whole operative clause including the comma figure.

## 2026-11-17 · enterprise_tech_leadership · pitfall

A collage header can pass the art director and the picture desk and still ship with an unreadable type block: on 2026-11-17 the paper_collage render put the kicker, title and hook over paper modules, and the hook line was buried behind the collage on the live page. The overlay's empty-spot search cannot see busy detail, and --anchor only moves the block, it does not clear the ground. Two consequences: (1) inspect the composited featured.jpg with vision before the push, not just the render, and (2) when the copy is three lines, commission a treatment whose composition leaves an open field (a cinematic still with a dark scrim beats a dense collage), because illustration_overlay.py re-derives its copy from the article each run and ignores a hook cleared in featured.json.
