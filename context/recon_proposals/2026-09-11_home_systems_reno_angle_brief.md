# Angle Brief: home_systems_reno — 2026-09-11

**Winner:** New York's flagship heat pump rebate just started paying homes that insulate first up to
double — and by March 2028, weatherization becomes a hard requirement, not a bonus.

**Scores:** N=9.0 A=9.0 S=8.5 → Composite=8.9

**Hook:** On September 1, 2026, NYS Clean Heat — the state program behind New York's largest heat pump
rebates — added a "Weatherized Tier" that raises the cold-climate heat pump rebate on National Grid
territory from $4,000 to $8,000 for a home that is already air-sealed and insulated (and $7,000 →
$12,000 when the old furnace is removed).

**Tension:** The federal heat pump tax credits (25C/25D) died on December 31, 2025. State and utility
rebates are now the entire savings game — and New York, the country's biggest heat pump rebate market,
is the first major program to formally reward the building envelope ahead of the equipment. The May 2025
utility order that authorized the program told administrators to make weatherization a prerequisite to
any Clean Heat rebate by March 1, 2028. The September 1 tier is the "bridge": insulate first, get paid
more now; get weatherized, or get shut out of the rebate entirely in 20 months.

**Target reader:** homeowner_pro (serious homeowner / builder — educated DIY-to-pro, wants payback math,
gotchas, and code/incentive reality).

**Single claim to defend:** New York's largest heat pump rebate now pays a materially higher incentive to
homes that are already weatherized — and weatherization becomes a hard gate for that rebate on March 1,
2028, so the envelope, not the equipment, is the first dollar a homeowner should spend.
