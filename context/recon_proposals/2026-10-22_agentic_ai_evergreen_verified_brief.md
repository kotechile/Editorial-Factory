## Gate rulings for the drafter

- **Do not state a derived difference.** 89.5% and 85.4% sit side by side in the table; the difference between them is a desk computation, not a source figure, so the article names both numbers and never their gap.
- **The ladder's provenance is the vendor's own benchmark.** Every 5.7% / 3.7% / 2.9% / 1.9% figure is Anthropic's measurement on Anthropic's corpus and must be attributed that way, never as an industry result.
- **"OpenAI's default" is Chroma's characterisation.** Chroma's report says OpenAI's documentation describes an 800-token chunk with 400-token overlap; OpenAI's own retrieval guide, fetched 2026-10-22, describes automatic chunking and states no size. Attribute the default to Chroma throughout.
- **The 9% recall spread and the 85.4%/89.5% rows come from one study** (n=5 retrieved chunks, one embedding model), not two independent ones.
- **FLAGGED, do not carry:** an OpenAI-side confirmation of the 800/400 default. The OpenAI retrieval guide fetched on 2026-10-22 documents automatic chunking with no size figure, so the claim stays Chroma-attributed.
- **REMOVED:** any claim that reranking or contextual chunking improves end-task answer quality. None of the three sources measures a finished task.
