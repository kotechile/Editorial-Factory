**Thesis:** A release gate's score is an estimate of one of two different questions, and the small custom suite that actually runs in CI is too small for the standard-error interval usually printed beside it — at N=100 a nominal 95% interval covered the truth only 92.5% of the time and at N=20 it ran past 0 and 1 — while the question a ship-or-hold call needs (generalized accuracy over similar cases) carries wider bars than the benchmark accuracy on the label; so size the suite to the smallest gap the decision depends on, state which question it answers, and report the interval, not the point score.
