## Drafting rulings

1. **The 60-minute run time is a vendor "up to" figure and must be attributed as one.** Consumer Reports' measured average is 45–50 minutes across the walk-behinds it tested (row 1). The article pairs the vendor's 60/60 for the ratio and states the measured band beside it; it never presents "up to 60" as a measurement.
2. **The 145-minute figure belongs to the standard charger (CH2100) specifically.** Row 7's ladder names the charger per column; the article names the standard charger in the same sentence and does not attach 145 minutes to EGO's chargers in general.
3. **The measured 45–50 minutes is a cross-brand average, not this mower's figure.** Write it as the average of the walk-behinds CR tested, never as the LM2135SP's measured run time.
4. **Rounded arithmetic only, with inputs shown.** The quarter-acre minutes (42 at 2 mph, 64 at 1.3, 27 at 3.1) and the 2.4× charge/run ratio must show the two inputs in the sentence; no other derived percentage is permitted.
5. **Do not claim any pack price, discount or kit configuration.** No price row was verified for this piece; the argument is time, not money, and a price invented for colour would be unsourced.
6. **No claim that the pack or charger is unique to this brand.** The ratio is arithmetic; EGO's ladder is the worked example, and the article says so.
