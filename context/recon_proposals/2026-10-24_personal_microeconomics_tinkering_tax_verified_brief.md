## Gate rulings for the drafter

- **F1 — the 8.6 is one link, not the chain.** Plex says the *chain* is unauthenticated; NVD and the KEV gloss both describe the 8.6 file-write stage as requiring admin privileges on its own. Write "four flaws that chain into unauthenticated code execution" and never "an 8.6 unauthenticated flaw". If the article names the 8.6, it must not attach it to the word "unauthenticated".
- **F2 — two dates for the proof of concept.** Plex states a public proof of concept was released on **2026-10-16**; the repository this run can verify was created **2026-10-18T05:00Z**. Cite Plex for the 16th and describe the repository as existing from the 18th. Do not assert that the repository *is* the October 16 artefact.
- **F3 — the catalogue date is 2026-10-20, not the 21st.** The registry's `dateAdded` is 2026-10-20 and CISA's alert URL is dated 2026/10/20; Field Effect dates it 2026-10-21 and The Hacker News was published 2026-10-21. Use 2026-10-20.
- **F4 — the interval that is safe to print.** Plex's public advisory is dated **2026-09-01**; the proof of concept **2026-10-16** — that is **45 days**, computed from the two dates. Do **not** say the fix "sat unapplied for 74 days": the "August 3-4" build date is Field Effect's, and Plex's own post says only "we recently released 1.43.3". Also safe: 2026-09-01 → 2026-09-09 is **8 days** (advisory to Shadowserver's count); 2026-10-16 → 2026-10-17 is **1 day** (PoC to first attack); 2026-10-20 → 2026-10-23 is **3 days** (listing to federal due date).
- **F5 — the 36,000 figure is a September snapshot.** It is Shadowserver's count of exposed, unpatched instances as of 2026-09-09, eight days after the advisory. Say "in September" or "eight days after the advisory" every time it appears; never present it as a current count, and never as a count of *compromised* servers.
- **F6 — what is actually known about victims.** Plex says "assume it may be compromised"; Ars and Field Effect describe a honeypot and observed payloads. No source states how many servers were successfully infected, so the article must not print an infection count, a dollar loss, or an electricity cost.
- **F7 — the mitigation is the vendor's, not the desk's advice.** Plex's own list (update to 1.43.3.10861; check codecs folder; rebuild from clean install; sign out of devices and change the password; treat reachable shares as exposed; turn off remote access if unneeded) is reportable as Plex's guidance. The desk must not tell the reader what to do.
- **F8 — no product recommendation.** Jellyfin, Emby or "switch to X" are not part of any verified source in this brief. The article may note that Plex's own forum thread on the ad behaviour exists only if it is sourced; it is not, so leave it out.
