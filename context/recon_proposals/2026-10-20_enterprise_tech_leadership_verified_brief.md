## Gate rulings for the drafter

1. **Do not describe the $3.5 trillion as a booked loss.** Gartner's own words are "could be exposed to impairment" and "if AI providers continue their current pace of capital spending" — a conditional forecast, not an event. The same applies to the $4 trillion through 2032.
2. **"Cease independent operations" is not "go bankrupt".** Row 4's verbatim must survive; write "cease independent operations", and if the piece glosses it, gloss it as consolidation or absorption, never as liquidation.
3. **The 12% is a hurdle rate Gartner assumes, not a measured industry return.** Attribute it ("the hurdle rate Gartner assumes for investment capital").
4. **The OCP study is commissioned by the Open Compute Project Foundation.** Every sentence using rows 7–8 must name IDC as the author and the foundation as the commissioner. It is not an independent measurement of the AI market.
5. **Authorised arithmetic only:** the gap between $8.5 trillion and $3.6 trillion (about $4.9 trillion), and $3.6 trillion as roughly 42% of $8.5 trillion. Nothing else may be derived — no per-GPU, per-year or per-operator figures, and no multiplication of the $1 trillion 2026 capex across years.
6. **Out-of-window figures may not carry an argument.** IDC's July 2026 tracker ($497 billion for 2026, $1.08 trillion in 2029), Synergy's Q2 number and the DORA report are dated background only.
7. **No invented operator names.** The piece may name Broadcom, Intel, Gartner and IDC because those are in the record; it may not name CoreWeave, Nebius or any other neocloud as "expected to fail" — Gartner's 80% is an aggregate, not a list.
