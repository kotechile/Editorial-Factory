## Binding rulings for the drafter

- **R1 — attribution.** Ring-0 is by Xiang Li, Aichun Huang, Zuyao Xu, Fasheng Miao and Zheli Liu (Nankai University and Tsinghua University). Show-Up is by Maosen Zhang, Jianshuo Dong, Yutong Zhang, Jie Zhang, Tianwei Zhang and Han Qiu (Tsinghua University, Nanyang Technological University, SiliconProspect AI). Never merge the two teams, never shorten either to "Tsinghua", never call Ring-0 a security-firm report.
- **R2 — "45 of 45" is a count, not a law.** Five runs per cell means the Wilson 95% lower bound is 0.57; the paper reports that no exception occurred in five runs. Write "in all 45 runs", never "always" or "everywhere".
- **R3 — the capability exists (FLAG).** Claude Code ships a `tools` field and the docs describe custom subagents as having "independent permissions"; the measured result is that the *default* is inheritance (45 of 45) and that the child's authority equals the parent's unless someone scopes it. Do not write "there is no way to scope a subagent". This is the article's honest counter-argument, not a footnote.
- **R4 — the arena is synthetic (FLAG).** Show-Up's ASR is judged by a judge model on 188 constructed tasks (Appendix D.1). Say "in a test arena", never "in production". The 0.73 is a mean over runs with ±.01.
- **R5 — 0.93 is not usable.** The abstract's "ASR of up to 0.93" is one attacker/supervisor pair in one table; the article's numbers are 0.73 and 0.87 (GPT-5.6-Sol) and 0.58 / 0.93 TSR (Gemini-3.6-Flash). Do not blend a TSR into an ASR sentence.
- **R6 — Ring-0's 65 is a count of missing mechanisms, not a percentage.** Write "65 of 116".
- **R7 — the tool-side fix (2611.11571) is a different experiment** (13 tasks, 12 tools, Qwen2.5-1.5B and 7B, 156 attack episodes per defense). It is not evidence about subagent scope; carry it only as the enforcement-point lesson.
- **R8 — no claim about the desk's own runtime.** The articles are about the reader's agent stack; the desk runs no multi-agent production system, and the paper's CLIs are not ours.
