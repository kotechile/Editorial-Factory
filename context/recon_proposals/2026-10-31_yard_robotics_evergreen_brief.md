**Thesis:** A robot mower's payback is set by the length of your mowing season, not by its price: the same $2,799.98 quarter-acre machine is a 2.4-year payback against the Northeast's 24-week, $1,176-a-year mowing bill and a 0.86-year payback against the Southeast's 45-week, $3,240 one — so the honest arithmetic is one division (machine price ÷ your region's annual mowing total), and the monthly "lawn care" averages that payback claims usually lean on are the wrong denominator because they describe a year of service a northern lawn never receives.
