## Angle it opens

The exit ramp from an unaffordable house has closed twice over: you cannot buy (pending sales at record lows, builder confidence at 25, 46% of builders cutting prices and it still does not clear the market) and you cannot cheaply hold-and-borrow (7.35% first-lien money, 7.47–8.48% second-lien money). For the pro homeowner who must fix a house they expected to sell or refinance out from under, the remodel capitalization trap is now priced: every deferred item is being converted from a "before we list" line item into a 7.5–8.5% liability that compounds. The story is the arithmetic of staying put — when the loan to fix the roof costs more per dollar than the mortgage it rides behind, the TCO decision (fix vs. defer vs. mechanical upgrade that pays an energy bill) is the only lever left.
