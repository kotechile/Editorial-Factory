6. **"Trade working capital" is Fastenal's own line**, not a cash conversion cycle this desk computed; the only CCC-adjacent figures quoted are the cash-conversion percentages the release states.
7. **No earnings-call quotes.** The only first-hand documents are the release, the 10-Q and the 8-K; executive quotes that exist only in trade coverage are barred from the body.
