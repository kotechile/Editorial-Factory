## Gate rulings (binding on the drafter)

1. **Do not claim a 24-hour detection latency.** KRGV's citable record is the *repeat cadence* ("every 24 hours, weekly, or biweekly"). BPUB's own Instagram post describes a "24 hours of uninterrupted water flow" trigger, but it is a social post and blocked as a citation; the drafter may say "the fastest repeat is once a day", never "the alert arrives only after 24 hours of flow".
2. **Do not claim the meter samples faster than the alert.** BPUB says "real-time" and "daily"; its actual interval is unpublished. The defensible point is that the *notification* is on a schedule, not that the meter is idle between messages.
3. **Do not claim LoRaWAN detects leaks faster than AMI.** LoRaWAN is a transport with a 51-byte payload; its reporting interval is set by the device and the network server. The claim is that the owner sets the interval, not that the radio is quicker.
4. **Do not claim a batteryless leak sensor exists.** The release notes name wall switches and a generic "sensor that lives off the light in the room"; no leak device is named. The capability is "a sensor can run without a battery", not "your leak sensor will".
5. **Scope every utility claim to Brownsville.** The 24-hour-to-biweekly cadence is BPUB's, stated by its spokesperson; it is not an industry standard and the piece must not generalise it to "utilities".
6. **Do not print one completion date.** April 2027 (BPUB page) and May 2027 (city progress report) both exist; write "spring 2027" or show both.
7. **No cost claim about local hardware.** This run verified no device price; the draft must not put a dollar figure on a monitor, valve or sensor.
8. **Germany's 5.9% figure is as of 30 June 2026** — if used at all, carry the as-of date; it is a runner-up, not an anchor.
9. **Authorised arithmetic only:** 52,000 + 58,000 = 110,000; "biweekly" = 14 days; 24 hours = one day. No gallons, no dollars, no percentages are computed in this piece.
10. **No hands-on claim.** This desk tested nothing; the piece reports published records.
