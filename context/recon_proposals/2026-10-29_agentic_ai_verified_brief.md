## Gate rulings for the drafter

- **Both anchors are primary and measured.** Anchor 1 is an empirical attack study across nine named harnesses; Anchor 2 is a runtime library evaluated on tau-bench's 50 airline tasks. Do not let either leg's claim migrate: Anchor 1 is about *message provenance across a summarisation step*, Anchor 2 is about *protocol state at tool dispatch*. The fused claim is that both are the same missing type.
- **87% is a floor.** Write "87% to 100%" in the body where the range fits, and let the headline carry the floor. Never write "87% of all agents" or "87% of compactions" — the rate is per harness, across five named harnesses.
- **Name the harnesses exactly as the paper does**, in the paper's own split (five flattening, four role-typed). No product outside those nine may be called vulnerable or safe.
- **The 13-of-50 and 29-of-128 figures are different bases** (tasks vs task-runs) and different models (gpt-4.1 vs Claude Haiku 5.5). Never merge them into "42 of 178" or a single rate.
- **"tau-bench's own reward did not detect most of these violations"** is the paper's sentence; keep the word "most", and do not claim it detected none.
- **CASEL is a simulation and says so.** Attribute the 45% figure to the simulator's assumptions, never to a measured deployment. The article must not claim a production saving.
- **The approval audit's numbers are attack-success and catch rates in scripted trials**, not field data. 0.30 → 0.98 is the catch rate with a trusted card; 0.45–0.60 flooding is against a modeled approver; 16% false refusal is partly a stimulus artifact.
- **No hands-on claim.** The desk ran no harness, forged no approval and read no source code; it read four papers, one release note and one changelog.
- **Acronyms to expand at first use:** large language model (LLM), application programming interface (API) if used, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) if the release note is quoted. The paper's own terms need one plain gloss each: "compaction" (summarising the running conversation and carrying the summary forward as memory), "provenance" (who said what), "protocol state" (which actions the interface permits right now).
- **Confirmed negative:** no publisher in this window has measured whether compaction laundering happens in production, and neither anchor paper reports a field incident. The article says the attack is measured in a lab setting and named harnesses, never that it has been observed in the wild.
