**Emergent Collision Point:** The budget is written as a share of infrastructure spend; the bill is written in actions whose count the agent's own design sets. Neither document can state it because each only sees its own unit: CloudZero can measure the share but not what makes an individual request cost one action or five, and Salesforce can publish a price per action but not the number of actions a buyer's workflow will take. Put together, the "guess" in the budget is not a forecasting weakness — it is the arithmetic of an action meter with an unfixed step count, and the only number that reconciles the two sides is actions per resolved request, which neither vendor's contract and neither survey reports.
