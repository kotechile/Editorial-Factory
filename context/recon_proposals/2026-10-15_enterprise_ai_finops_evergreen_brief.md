**Thesis:** The sticker price of a model tier does not price the work — output and reasoning tokens are billed at a multiple of the input rate and the reasoning span is invisible in the response, so a tier priced 50% cheaper per token can end up 57% more expensive per resolved task, and the swap has to be measured on your own traffic before it is signed.
