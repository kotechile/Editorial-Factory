## Gate rulings for the drafter

- **Dual-anchor gate:** PASS. Leg A carries 6 VERIFIED claims (rows 1–6, 9, 10); Leg B carries 5 (rows 11–15). No leg falls below the 2-VERIFIED floor. 0 REMOVED.
- **The 10× is Anchor A's own claim, in Anchor A's own words, and it is a comparison against Revolut's *prior invoice*.** Do not write "self-hosting is ten times cheaper than Anthropic" as a market fact. Write "about a tenth of what it had been paying OpenAI and Anthropic [1]".
- **The price series (row 17) is secondhand twice over** — Tom's Hardware reporting The Register, on a basis the piece does not disclose. Use it as an index for the arithmetic, name it as the index, and put its provenance in The catch. Do not describe it as Anthropic's list price.
- **The 2.85× is the desk's arithmetic on that index (row 18), not a figure either source states.** Show the inputs once, in `By the numbers`, and use the result in the lead and the title. Do not restate the division in the prose.
- **Apple's figures come from people Bloomberg spoke to; Apple declined to comment (rows 11–15).** Every Apple sentence in the body carries that provenance at first use ("people Bloomberg spoke to", "Apple did not respond to a request for comment").
- **Row 14 is the honest counterweight and may not be dropped:** few engineers were using the full $300, so the saving from the cut may be small. It belongs in The catch, not in a rhetorical aside.
- **Row 7 (the confound) may not be dropped either:** Revolut's gain may partly reflect removing the usage limits rather than the model, and its comparison does not isolate the change. Both caveats live in The catch, together.
- **No claim about OpenAI or Anthropic cutting prices for competitive reasons.** Neither source states a cause for the 77% fall.
- **Do not use the Forrester budget-share figure** (20% of technology spend in 2027, 22% positive operating-profit impact): it was anchored cross-desk on 2026-11-03 by `2026-11-03_your-ai-roi-28-rising-28-stalled.md`.
