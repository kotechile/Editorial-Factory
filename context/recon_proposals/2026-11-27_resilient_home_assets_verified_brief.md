**Rulings for the drafter**
1. The in-window event is ARMA's pushback (2026-11-09); the bulletin itself (2026-09-29) is out-of-window provenance — say "released in September" and never claim IBHS announced the rule in November.
2. The limit is an **eligibility** limit for FORTIFIED designation, not a roof-replacement mandate, not an insurer rule, and does not apply to metal or tile roofs (rows 2, 4, and the designations page: "It does not mean your asphalt shingle roof must be replaced after 15 years").
3. Designation term (5 years) and designation eligibility (up to 15 years) are different clocks — never merge them.
4. The ARMA quote, the Florida statute account, the state count (Florida, Texas, Virginia), Security First's quote and the Alabama share are all read through Insurance Journal (a trade paper quoting the parties) — attribute every one of them; do not present them as this desk's reading of the statute text.
5. The 100,000-designation figure is a May 2026 milestone (dated background), usable only as scale context, never as a November event.
6. Do not generalise Florida's statutory bar to other states; the article's tension is that 47 states have no such bar.
