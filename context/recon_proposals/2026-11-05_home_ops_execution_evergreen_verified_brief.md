## Notes for the drafter

- **The load-bearing distinction is role, not paperwork.** None of these sections says an owner needs a licence; each removes the owner from a licensing statute. The claim that carries the piece is that the *exemption has conditions*, and that the role it confers is the general contractor's — supervision, employment, insurance and the resale window.
- **Two labour tests, and they are not the same test.** California's DIY route (row 2) requires the owner or the owner's *employees* with wages as sole compensation, so an unlicensed sole-proprietor subcontractor breaks it. Florida's route (rows 8, 11) requires direct onsite supervision and employment, which is what pulls in tax withholding and workers' compensation. Do not merge them into one "you must do the work yourself".
- **Florida names the kickback pattern itself (row 10).** That sentence is the article's best warrant and should be quoted as the state's own disclosure, not as the desk's suspicion.
- **The resale rules carry different legal weights and the prose must keep them apart:** California rebuttable at one year, conclusive at five or more (rows 5, 6); Florida a presumption (row 12); Oregon prima facie from non-occupancy (row 15); Washington a bar on the exemption itself under twelve months (row 17). Never write "you lose the exemption" for the California or Florida presumptions — a presumption is evidence, and the burden is the point.
- **Oregon's permitted-work clause (row 14) is the sharpest line in the set** and it contradicts the usual advice: hiring licensed trades is not enough where a building permit is required.
- **Washington's floor (row 16) is not a DIY threshold.** The same subsection excludes work that is part of a larger operation, so a small kitchen inside a larger remodel is not casual, minor or inconsequential.
- **Figures are budgeted across sections:** `12 months` and `$75,000` are the only figures the shape gate counts, and each may appear in at most two body sections. Put `four structures`, `three structures` and `five hundred dollars` in the numbers block; the resale section uses the presumptions' weights, not their dates again.
- **No price anywhere (row 20), no hands-on claim (row 21).** If the body wants the reader to weigh a saving, it says only that the fee is on their own invoice.
