## Flags and drafting constraints

- **FLAGGED (scope):** rows 1–6 are Florida law and row 7–9 are Washington rules. Neither is national. The piece must say who each binds and tell the reader to ask what their own state's prompt-pay rule is. Printing "60 days" as a general rule would be a factual error.
