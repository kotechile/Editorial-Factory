## Rulings for the drafter (binding)

1. **The 6.4 points are the workflow plus its worker instructions, never topology alone.** The paper's own limitation says "because source models also write the instructions given to workers, its gains may combine better organization with task knowledge conveyed in those instructions." Write the gain as what travelled — a workflow and the instructions it carries — and never as "structure is worth 6.4 points".
2. **Keep the setting attached to every Astra number.** 30.80% is xhigh effort in the main table (#3); 27.34% is max effort in the transfer experiment (#10). They are two settings of one system; never blend them, and never call Astra "the second-best model" (it ranks fifth of the 22 configurations on overall resolved rate).
3. **No variance, no "proves".** The paper states "Each configuration is evaluated once per task, so differences between closely ranked configurations are reported without variance estimates", each model ran in a single harness ("differences between the Claude and GPT systems therefore reflect the model and its harness jointly"), and content and 3-D deliverables are graded by an LLM judge with no measured human agreement. Say measured, name the benchmark, and do not generalise the Claude-versus-GPT delegation difference to all models or all harnesses.
4. **The transfer subset is 32 tasks and two source models.** Never write "on all 145 tasks" for the 6.41 / 6.27 averages, and never imply the four executors are frontier peers — they are the weaker systems the transfer was meant to lift.
5. **SAP is announced, not shipped.** The Enterprise AI Gateway is planned for Q1 2027, the agent-to-agent gateway is in phased general availability, and Joule Work/Joule Studio are rolling out phased. Never "SAP routes agents through", "SAP enforces", or "the gateway consolidates" in the present tense; write "will consolidate" or "is designed to".
6. **"More than 70 AI models" is the vendor's own count** and refers to what Joule Studio builders can choose from — not to models the fabric orchestrates at run time.
7. **No derived arithmetic.** Do not compute a cost per point, a percentage improvement between 34.11% and 36.95%, or a "worth X dollars" figure; the paper prints the multiples and the per-task costs, and the article may repeat them but not build new ones except the plainly stated 3.31-point and 4.4× gap, both of which the paper prints.
