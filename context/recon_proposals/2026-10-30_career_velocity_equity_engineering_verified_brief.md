## Gate rulings the drafter must honour

1. **"50.1% of the vote", never "50.1% of the company".** Class F is a voting instrument with no economic rights; the founders' economic stake is separate (and, per Fortune, they have pledged to give away 80% of their equity — if that is used it is Fortune's sentence, attributed).
2. **Never write that employees get "no votes".** Employees receive Class A shares, one vote each (row 4), plus Class E (row 3). The accurate claim is that their votes cannot change an outcome. The vivid, defensible version is the *asymmetry*: their voting share is earned at one share per six months, halved each year after a non-cause exit, redeemed for no consideration — while the founders' 50.1% sits in one share that new issuance cannot dilute.
3. **"Limits the likelihood of an *unsolicited* tender offer" — not "no tender offers, ever".** The filing says the structure makes an unsolicited approach less likely; a company-organized tender is a different animal and Anduril's is exactly that. Writing "Anthropic will never run a tender" is a claim the document does not make.
4. **$23.7 billion is unrecognized stock-based compensation expense, not cash and not the market value of what employees hold.** It is measured at grant-date fair value and is the amount still to be recognized over ~2.1 years. Never divide it by headcount, never call it "employee wealth", never call it a payout.
5. **No Anthropic share price, no Anthropic valuation, no share counts.** The preliminary prospectus prints blanks for the price range, the number of shares offered, and the plan reserves. The piece must never imply one. If a valuation is mentioned at all it is as the thing recruiters quote, not as a figure this desk computed.
6. **Attribute Anduril's numbers to their own sourcing.** $94.80, the $90 billion valuation and the confirmation are CNBC's; the $1.3 billion cap and the 16 countries are "according to the report" (Bloomberg, relayed). Do not upgrade the cap to Anduril's own statement.
7. **Class E is not yet issued** — "no issuance of shares of Class E common stock prior to the completion of this offering". Write the mechanic in the future tense the filing uses.
8. **Do not call the Long-Term Benefit Trust the owner of the company.** It holds Class T: no economic rights, and the power to elect a majority of the board. That distinction is the article's whole point and inverting it destroys it.
