## Drafter rulings (carry these into the draft)

1. **Do not say the GFCI is legally required.** CPSC's own language is that the units "lack" the device;
   the agency treats its absence as a defect but no cited page states a statute or standard by number.
   Write "the device CPSC names as the missing part", never "the standard requires".
2. **Do not claim that a licensing model legally transfers or escapes recall liability.** Nothing in the
   record says that. The licensing transition is a stated portfolio decision; the recall record is what
   happens when the party behind the name cannot be reached. Keep them as two facts that collide, and put
   the inference in a question or an observation, never in a legal assertion.
3. **The 2% figure is a sales headwind, not a price cut.** SBD's release says the licensing transition
   reduced net sales by 2 percentage points. Pricing (−3%) is the separate, explicit price change. Do not
   merge them.
4. **Benchmark (37,667 snow throwers) may appear only as the third remedy in a comparison.** The
   cold-rating thesis was published on 2026-10-31 and must not be re-argued.
5. **No price for a replacement GFCI adapter.** No cited source prices one; the article may say the device
   exists at the plug on machines that carry it and name the plug as the thing to check, but it may not
   invent a cost for adding one.
6. **The $300m Excel figure belongs to FY2026 revenue, not to a sale price.** SBD did not disclose terms.
7. **Generac is the importer of record on the DR Power units, not the designer of the trimmer mower's
   engine.** Write "imported by Generac Power Systems" only.
8. **One incident on PowRyte, not a pattern of injuries.** The one incident is "of a unit lacking a GFCI";
   no injury is reported in the record.
9. **SEK figures must be given in kronor**, and any dollar comparison is forbidden — no exchange rate is
   cited anywhere in this brief.
