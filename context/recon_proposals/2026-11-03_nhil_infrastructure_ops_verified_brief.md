## FLAGGED (carry the caveat; do not state as record)
1. **The root cause is the operator's own account.** Google published the final report on 27 October; there is no independent audit, and the configuration finding is the party that owns the equipment describing its own failure. The body may report it as Google's finding, never as an externally confirmed one.

The article must not claim that utilities cannot tell a shed from a backup switch in general — only that the electrical record at the fence is not sufficient on its own, which is the paper's own conclusion (B4).
