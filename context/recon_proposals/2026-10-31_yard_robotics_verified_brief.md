**Gate rulings for the drafter.**
1. Never write that the FCC "banned robot mowers". The rule bars **new models** from equipment authorization; models authorized before 2026-07-28 may keep being imported, marketed and sold, and owners may keep using what they own. Consumer Reports' "banned the sale" sentence is the popular gloss and may be quoted as the gloss, immediately corrected from the FCC's own FAQ.
2. Never name a manufacturer as refused, denied or blocked. The FCC publishes approvals, not applications. The only defensible negative is: "as of the register's 2026-10-23 update, the only consumer yard machines on it are Husqvarna's four platforms".
3. Do not convert the seven approvals into "seven robot-mower makers" — six of the seven entries are indoor cleaners or industrial AGV/AMR platforms; say "seven approvals, one of them for a yard machine".
4. The Q3 loss is a **group** figure, not a robot-mowing figure. Husqvarna does not publish robot-mowing revenue in this release; the robot-mowing read is qualitative (the CEO's paragraph), and the -334m must not be attributed to robotics.
5. Do not assert a measured price war. "Competitive headwinds" is the company's own phrase; no in-window source publishes competitor prices, so no price comparison may be written.
6. Permitted derived arithmetic (printed endpoints only): 4 of the 7 approvals dated 2026-10-16 or later; the 65%-to-75% domestic-content step lands in 2029, i.e. two boundaries the reader can check.
7. Price range for the category comes from CR ($800 to more than $2,500) and must carry its source; no other price may be invented.
8. The "works with the borders still yours" line is another outlet's opinion — attribute it, never state it as the desk's finding.

**Reader decision.** Whether to buy an already-authorized robot mower in the current season or wait for a 2027 platform that has cleared the approval gate — and what the wait actually buys (choice of new models) versus what it costs (the current line-up is not the scarce thing; new platforms are).
