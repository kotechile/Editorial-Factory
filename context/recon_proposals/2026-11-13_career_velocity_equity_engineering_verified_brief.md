## Rulings the drafter must honour
1. **The two prices are different instruments.** Leg A's number is a *price per share agreed in a financing*; Leg B's is a *price per share cleared in a trade*, each measured against that company's own last primary round. Never average them, never present one as a check on the other.
2. **Name the denominator.** "Companies on Carta", "the median trade on Forge's marketplace", "in September". No sentence may generalise either to "the market" or "startups".
3. **The tails are noisy by the source's own admission** (#10). Say that where the 58%-to-15% move is stated; do not present it as a clean repricing of the top decile.
4. **The bottom of the distribution improved** (#8). The 10th percentile's discount narrowed from 87% to 51%; a piece that reports only the top-end collapse and the median is selecting its evidence. Carry it.
5. **The discount is to the round, not to profit.** A 9% discount does not make a grant worthless, and it does not mean the company is overvalued. It means the mark and the clearing price differ by nine points on the median name that traded.
6. **No AMT argument.** #12 is context for why a holder cares about a *price* (a cash tax can precede any sale); `2026-10-09_post-termination-option-clock-tax` already owns the AMT thesis, so the 2027 numbers may appear in at most one sentence and may not carry an argument. Do not state the 42% marginal figure.

8. **No recommendation.** No "sell", "don't exercise", "negotiate for X". The piece reports what the two prices did and what a holder can compute from them.
