## Notes for the drafter

- Do not claim that every AMI customer can download their data. The EIA defines AMI as delivering usage to the customer at least once a day; the DOE and Alliance figures describe programs, and 40 million live users against 60 million committed is the honest gap. Write "where the utility runs a Green Button program", never "your utility must".
- Do not claim a 15-minute interval. The EIA floor is hourly; finer intervals exist at some utilities but no page fetched this round states one, so the piece's unit is the hour and the row count that follows from it.
- Do not price the monitor. No retail page answered the verifier (Emporia and Shelly both unreachable, `sparkfun.com` fetches a CT sensor but no whole-home kit), so the decision is settled on information, not on a break-even — and the piece says so rather than inventing a payback.
- The local-first conclusion is the export, not the hardware: a standard-format file the owner keeps is an archive; a portal is a view. Keep that distinction in the closing section and keep it cheap — one rule, no sermon.
