**Thesis:** A replacement-cost roof claim is paid twice — the first check is the depreciated value — and the withheld depreciation is only ever paid on proof that the work was completed inside a window the policy has to grant, which is why cashing the first check and closing the claim quietly converts a replacement-cost policy into an actual-cash-value one.
