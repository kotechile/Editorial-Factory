## Gate rulings for the drafter
1. **The collision is the date, not a villain.** Louisiana's implementation deadline (2027-01-01) and the 1 January 2027 reinsurance renewals fall on the same day. State that plainly; do not assert that either side knew about the other.
2. **Attribute the 10% correctly.** It is KBW's expectation for property catastrophe excess-of-loss rates, reported by trade press, not a published rate and not a figure from Marsh Re. The Marsh Re document supports the direction (capital +6%, premium base −7%, "continued property rate softening"), not the size.
3. **Do not convert reinsurance percentages into a homeowner's premium.** There is no sourced pass-through ratio in this record. Any sentence implying "a 10% reinsurance cut means 10% off your bill" is fabricated; the honest statement is that the two percentages apply to different bases (rate-on-line vs. the hurricane slice of a retail premium).
4. **Arithmetic the desk is allowed to do, with the inputs printed in the sentence:** a percentage of a slice is smaller than the same percentage of the bill — e.g. "49% of a $2,000 hurricane portion is $980" must be introduced as a hypothetical slice, labelled as an example, and never presented as a market average. Do not compute a payback period: no source here prices the incremental cost of a FORTIFIED roof for a single-family home in Louisiana.
5. **Do not call the grant guaranteed.** The lottery is closed and future rounds are unannounced; the $10,000 figure is a cap limited to construction costs.
6. **No claim about what carriers will do with the money.** Regulation 136 requires a discount on the hurricane portion; nothing in the record says how the rest of the premium is repriced. Any such sentence is the writer's reading and must be labelled as one.
7. **Synthesis frontmatter is required:** `synthesis: true` with both anchors under `sources:` — the LDI benchmarks URL and the Marsh Re 2027 Renewal Outlook URL.
