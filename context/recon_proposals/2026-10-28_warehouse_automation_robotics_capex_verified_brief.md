3. **65,000 hours is company-wide** ("across its commercial operations and customer programs"), not GXO-only and not per site. Never pair it with a single site's tote count to imply a rate.
4. **10,000 robots is production capacity, not output**, and it is Agility's factory, not GXO's fleet. Write "designed for annual production capacity of up to 10,000 robots".
5. **35-45% and 10-15% are AutoStore estimates** and both must carry that attribution in the same sentence. The source says "AutoStore estimates this can help reduce unplanned downtime by 35-45%".
6. **40-60% is a share of consumer e-commerce orders that are single-line**, not a share of any operator's volume, and not a labour saving.
7. **540 order lines per hour is "at the workstation"** for pre-picked orders — not a site throughput, and not a comparison to a picker's rate.
8. **"Lower-commitment Robotics-as-a-Service" is AutoStore's own phrase.** Quote it as a description, never as a contract term the operator can rely on.
9. **Cutter & Buck's grid figures are barred from this article** (50,502 bins / 32 robots / 1,256 / 1,284 bins an hour) — published by this desk on 2026-10-21.
10. **Do not write "two years and four months".** The RaaS agreement is dated June 2024; write "more than two years" or name both dates and let the reader do the arithmetic.
11. The interval that may be stated explicitly is 2026-10-13 → 2026-10-26 = **13 days**.
