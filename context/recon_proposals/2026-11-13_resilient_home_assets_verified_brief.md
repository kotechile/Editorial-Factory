## Gate rulings for the drafter

1. **Do not claim any of the 50,000 are FAIR Plan policyholders.** The CDI release never says so. Leg B is used for the *market* condition, not for the composition of the moratorium population.
2. **The clock's start date is 2026-10-15, and it is set by the declaration, not the bulletin.** State it as the CDI states it ("one year from the date of the Governor's emergency declaration") and give the declaration date from Cal OES. The 26 days already spent when the order issued is the article's own arithmetic and must be labelled as such.
3. **The declaration is a debris-cleanup declaration.** Cal OES: it "clears the way for state agencies to expedite the safe removal and disposal of hazardous debris." Do not describe it as a fire-suppression or evacuation emergency; the fire was out on 2026-08-01.
4. **Say "12 structures", not "12 homes".** The proclamation and Cal OES both say structures. The pre-fire evacuation figure (600 homes) exists only in the PDF proclamation, so leave the evacuation counts at the qualitative "prompted mandatory evacuations" that Cal OES states.
5. **The Gann Fire is a comparison, not a second anchor.** 10,339 acres and more than 50 structures, Calaveras County, with its own declaration — use it to show that the declaration, not the acreage, is what triggers a county-wide freeze. Do not imply the November bulletin's Calaveras coverage derives from the Gann declaration; the CDI release attributes the order to the Dove Fire.
6. **The FAIR Plan new-business decline is an average-monthly comparison across fiscal years, not a single month.** Write "a 27% decrease in the average month" and keep the fiscal-year frame (October 2025 – September 2026).
7. **No insurance advice.** The tactical section reports what carriers, the Department and the plan are doing. It does not tell a reader to shop, file, or call.
8. **Only use the figures above.** The FAIR Plan's rate increase (29.1%, effective 2026-10-15) was anchored by this vertical on 2026-09-25 and is not part of this argument — if mentioned at all it is a single corroborating clause, never a load-bearing number.
