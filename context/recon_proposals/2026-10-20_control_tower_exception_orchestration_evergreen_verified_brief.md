**Dropped / flagged:** nothing removed. No claim in this brief depends on a vendor page, a PDF, or a client-rendered table.
**Currency note for the drafter:** eCFR prints its own as-of line — title 19 "up to date as of 10/16/2026", title 21 "up to date as of 10/16/2026" (last amended 10/14/2026). Say "as of October 2026" rather than implying the rule is new.
**Do not write:** that a control tower is legally the recordkeeper of a customs entry. The obligation in 19 CFR 163.2 attaches to the party required to make, keep and render the record; who that is depends on who filed. The draft says the tower usually *holds* the data, not that it carries the duty.
