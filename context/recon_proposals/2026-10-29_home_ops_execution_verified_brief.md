## Gate rulings for the drafter

1. **Allegations only.** These are charges, not convictions. The case is open; no plea had been entered as of 2026-10-14; the settlement conference is set for 2027-01-12. Every sentence about the Paulhus brothers or Anchored Tiny Homes carries "charged", "alleged" or "prosecutors say". Never write "stole", "defrauded", "took" or "guilty".
2. **Do not say the law "doubles the units".** The guaranteed total on a single-family lot stays four homes. AB 956 swaps one of them: a second detached cottage replaces the in-house junior ADU, which is no longer guaranteed once two detached units are built (§ 66323(b)). The correct sentence is about **which** units, not how many.
3. **The 800 square feet is a local option, not a state limit.** Write it as a cap a city may impose.
4. **Four-foot setbacks are the trigger for ministerial approval**, not a promise the plan fits. Fire separation between two structures on one lot, and fire-department access, can still govern placement — the ADU statute does not set building-to-building distance.
5. **The $1,000 / 10 percent rule is a lesser-of pair.** On any ADU-sized contract the operative cap is $1,000; the 10 percent bites only below a $10,000 contract price. Never print the pair as one number ("10 percent or $1,000"), and never present it as new — it is the board restating existing home-improvement payment law.
6. **No derived arithmetic.** Do not multiply a per-unit figure into a two-unit project total, do not compute a percentage saving, and do not turn the two named prepayments into a market price. The only arithmetic the article may do is naming intervals between dated events, and those must be computed, not estimated: the licence was revoked in November 2024 and the charges were reported on 2026-10-09 (KCRA) — 23 months, "nearly two years", never "two years" flat.
7. **The 200 complaints are cumulative**, filed before the revocation and after it; do not present them as complaints received in the last 30 days.
8. **Acronyms.** "Accessory dwelling unit (ADU)" and "junior accessory dwelling unit (JADU)" expanded on first use; "Contractors State License Board (CSLB)" expanded on first use. No bare all-caps tokens.
