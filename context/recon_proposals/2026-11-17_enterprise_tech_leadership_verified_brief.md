## Gate rulings for the drafter

1. **The claim is a P&L location, not a price.** The article may not re-argue that token prices fell and
   bills did not — `enterprise_ai_finops` published that on this front on 2026-11-12. The price movement
   survives only as one corroborating clause in a single sentence, with no argument resting on it and no
   figure attached to it beyond "cheaper".
2. **"Cost of revenue" is an accounting classification the page asserts, and the draft may assert it only
   with the page's own words nearby** — "82.0% of classified spend ran production" and the page's line
   that "when an AI feature goes from pilot to product, its spend moves out of the 18% and into the 82%".
   Do not claim that gross margin moved by a specific amount; no source measures that.
3. **Both the 82.0% and the 18% carry their base.** Median, 70 companies, complete environment tagging.
   Do not write "82% of companies" or "18% of budget" without that qualifier in the same paragraph.
4. **The gate-versus-meter numbers (27%, 20%, 7%) are one panel's self-report.** Attribute to
   CloudZero's FY2027 survey of 252 finance leaders in the same sentence as the figure; no causal verb.
5. **Gartner's numbers are forecasts and the draft says so.** "$4,050bn" may be written as "Gartner
   forecasts $4,050bn"; "$4 trillion" is the release's own rounding of the same table and both may appear
   as long as the table total is the one used for any arithmetic.
6. **The OpenRouter figures are platform data published as a named guest analysis** (Peter Walker, Head of
   Insights at OpenRouter, in OnlyCFO's Newsletter, 2026-10-22, data to late September). Name the platform
   and the date; do not write "OpenRouter says" as though it were a survey.
7. **Derived arithmetic must be shown, not asserted**: the $1.3 trillion gap is a subtraction of two
   published totals, and the 18% is 100 minus a published median. Both are the desk's arithmetic and the
   copy says so inline ("by subtraction", "the remaining").
8. **No figure may be attached to a company, vendor or product that the sources do not name.** The
   article has no case study in it: no named enterprise, no "one company we spoke to".
9. **Nothing about margins, headcount or layoffs.** Neither source measures either; an AI-cost article
   that reaches for a headcount cut is inventing.
10. **The 9.8% and 30.1% intensity tiers are panel shares, not market shares** — "of CloudZero's 430-company
    panel", never "of enterprises".
