## Rules for the drafter

- The 36-foot exception is the spine: at 35 feet FHWA's four-axle group is allowed 65,500 pounds, and at 36 feet the exception permits 34,000 per tandem (68,000). The step is 2,500 pounds for one foot of wheelbase.
- Never present 34,000 or 80,000 as a universal legal limit. They are Interstate maxima, states may not enforce lower (row 9), and permits (row 11) and grandfathers sit outside them. Name the Interstate in the sentence.
- The 12,000-pound steering figure is arithmetic (80,000 − 34,000 − 34,000), not a rule; say so.
- Do not print a trailer tare. Any payload example must show its inputs and label the tare as the reader's own registration weight.
- Do not claim a state's fine schedule or tolerance practice; the sources state only that no allowance above 5 percent may be applied and that no-fine thresholds are unauthorized.
- The 5 percent allowance on an 80,000-pound load is 4,000 pounds — derived arithmetic, printed with its inputs.
