## Gate rulings (binding on the drafter)

1. **32 is not universal.** It is the ZHA/Zigpy child table on a current coordinator; the 20/21 numbers belong to a different stack on last-generation firmware. The disagreement between sources *is* the story — never present one number as "the" Zigbee limit.
2. **Print the FAQ's 55 exactly as the page prints it, and label the 60 as this article's recomputation** from the page's own 20. Do not "correct" the quote and do not merge the two into one figure.
3. **No 65,000-node claim, no "up to 100 devices", no vendor spec numbers.** Anything the brief does not carry is `[NEEDS-SOURCE]`.
4. **The unavailable timeout and the signal band are diagnostics, not proof.** ZHA itself says not to judge received signal or link quality on their own, so any sentence quoting -80 dBm keeps that caveat nearby.
5. **Capacity is not coverage.** C3 is load-bearing: the arithmetic says how many devices a table admits, not how far the radio reaches.
6. **No product recommendations and no prices** — this brief carries neither, so the article names no brand and quotes no dollar figure.
7. **Model codes stay out of the body** (`CC2530`, `CC2531`, `CC2652` fail the acronym gate as bare `CC`): say "a last-generation coordinator" in prose; the Sources lines keep the specifics.
