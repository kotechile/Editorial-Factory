## What the drafter must not do

- Do not write that the paper measured production crash recovery. Leg 1 is a streaming/revision benchmark; leg 2's runtime does execute deterministic rollback trajectories, and the brief's prose must keep the two settings apart.
- Do not call the Absorber "more accurate". Its quality is statistically indistinguishable from full restart (3.07 vs 3.17), which is exactly why the waste figure is the story.
- Do not convert `14.6×` into a percentage or a cost. It is a ratio of discarded acts per run (0.78 vs 11.41) that the paper states as 14.6×.
- Do not present `45/45` as a security number. It is the count of risk-bearing effects intercepted before commit, out of 45 evaluated workflows.
- Do not treat the Temporal 51,200-event cap as a general agent-runtime rule; it is one runtime's documented limit and must be attributed to it.
- Do not merge the PostgreSQL rows into "two-phase commit is unavailable". The feature exists and works; it is off by default and its identifier is capped at 200 bytes.
- The 24-hour idempotency-key window is **out of scope** for this piece — `2026-10-14_one-approval-two-payments` already owns it.
