## Derived arithmetic the drafter may use (labelled as arithmetic, from the figures above)

- 84 GB ÷ 32 GB = **2.625** → "about 2.6 times the video memory on the same core count" (A1 + A3).
- $4,999 ÷ 64 GB = **$78.11 per GB**; $6,950 ÷ 128 GB = **$54.30 per GB**; at launch $3,999 ÷ 128 GB = **$31.24 per GB** → the 128 GB model's price per gigabyte rose **74%** ($6,950 ÷ $3,999), and today's 64 GB SKU costs about **2.5x** per GB what the 128 GB model did at launch (B5).
- Nebius per-tier increases from its own table: H100 **+16.9%**, H200 **+20.0%**, B200 **+18.9%**, B300 **+21.0%** (B2).

## Discrepancy to record (fact_check §3 — the table is the claim of record)

`getdeploying.com` prints two different "up" figures on the same page: the headline summary and its own FAQ both say cloud GPU rental prices are up **8%** over the year, while the H100-specific FAQ answer says the H100 is up **16%** over 12 months. These are not in conflict — the 8% is the median across 22 models, the 16% is the H100 alone — but the drafter must not merge them into one number. Also note the site's four-week figure appears as both "+1.5%" (median across 25 models) and "+4.0%" (H100 alone); attribute each to its own subject.
