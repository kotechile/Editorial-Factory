## Gate rulings for the drafter

1. **The 20%–30% is a forecast for 2029, not a current cost.** Write it as Gartner's prediction with the year attached. Never present it as what manufacturers pay now, and never convert "most manufacturers" into a percentage.
2. **Gartner published no sample size with this release.** Do not invent a survey of N leaders; the release is a prediction plus analyst commentary, and the article must not dress it as a measured survey.
3. **Manhattan is one vendor.** Its revenue mix is evidence about one company's business model, never about "the planning software market". The article's claim is structural — a mix-shift that other listed vendors report too — and it must stay inside what one filing supports.
4. **Services revenue is not planning services.** The 10-K definition spans implementation, training and application managed services across the whole portfolio. Never write "planning services" or "forecasting services" as if the line were planning-only.
5. **The $21,000 is arithmetic.** State it as the desk's subtraction from the income statement's thousands, because the release itself rounds both lines to $134.4M. Do not attribute the subtraction to Manhattan.
6. **Two different years, two different directions.** Fiscal 2025 services revenue fell 4.3% ($525.5M → $503.0M); the Q3 2026 services line grew 1.1%. The article may use both, each dated, and must not merge them into one trend claim.
7. **RPO +25% is year on year.** Quarter on quarter it rises 4.7% ($2.474B → $2.590B). Say which base any RPO figure is measured from.
8. **Do not claim the services line pays for energy modelling.** No source says that. The defensible claim is narrower: the configuration work is the line that carries site-specific model building, and it is the optional line that gets deferred when budgets tighten.
9. **Do not claim Gartner names energy management software or a vendor.** It names "advanced energy management capabilities" and grid constraints; nothing more.
10. **Omit Manhattan's June 2026 6% headcount reduction.** It is a real restructuring disclosure, but this vertical published a headcount-and-planning argument on 2026-11-05 and repeating it here would turn a supply-side story into a labour story.
