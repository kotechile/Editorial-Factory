## Gate rulings for the drafter

1. **Coverage figure — use Amazon's, not Ring's.** Amazon's own explainer says "Over 90% of people in the U.S live in an area with Sidewalk coverage"; Ring's Sidewalk marketing page says "Covers approximately 95% of the country". These are two first-party glosses of the same network and they disagree. **The article uses "more than 90% of the U.S. population" and never prints 95%.**
2. **The Matter absence is a document fact, not an intention.** Write "the release names Ring, Echo and Alexa" — never "Ring skipped Matter on purpose" or "the lock will never support Matter".
3. **No price.** Ring's product page carries no price and the release carries none; trade-press figures ($249.99) are secondary. The article names no dollar amount for either lock.
4. **The 0.15% is a bandwidth cap on the Bridge donor, not a cost to the lock owner.** Do not render it as a fee or a bill.
5. **The iCloud+ point is a substitution, not an absence of subscription.** Apple removes the camera subscription and moves storage into iCloud+; write it that way.
6. **Authorised arithmetic:** the six-day interval (2026-10-07 → 2026-10-13) and the count of four meanings of local are the only derived quantities permitted. No other subtraction, ratio or interval may appear.
7. **Dual-anchor gate PASS:** leg A carries 6 VERIFIED claims across two hosts (apple.com, lg.com); leg B carries 6 VERIFIED claims across three hosts (aboutamazon.com, blog.ring.com, ring.com). 0 REMOVED, 0 FLAGGED (B6 carried as a framed gap with its ruling).
