## Gate rulings the drafter must honour

1. **Two regimes, never one.** Post-July 4, 2025 stock: 50/75/100 at three/four/five years (rows 1, 2, 8). Earlier stock: five years for 100% (row 3). A sentence that says "QSBS is a 100% exclusion after five years" is true only of the older regime and will mislead every reader who exercises in 2026.
2. **The 28% is a maximum, and it is not the ordinary rate.** Write "a maximum 28% rate" as row 20 does. Never write "28% flat", never write "the QSBS rate", and never fold the 3.8% surtax into it as though it were statutory.
3. **15.9% / 7.95% / 84.1% / $159,000 are the run's arithmetic.** Show the multiplication (50% taxable × 31.8%) in the same sentence or bullet, exactly as the brief's table does. Do not present any of them as a published figure.
4. **The $75,000,000 test is measured twice** — at all times before the issuance and immediately after it, counting the money the issuance itself raises (row 9). That is why a large round can close the door: the cash lands on the balance sheet in the same instant the shares are issued.
5. **Qualification is decided at issuance, not at exit** (rows 10, 11, 13). The corporation must be a C corporation and a qualified small business on the day the shares are issued, and must stay an operating business for substantially all of the holding period.
6. **The 10-times-basis alternative rarely binds for employees.** At $15,000,000 ÷ 10, the alternative only overtakes the dollar cap above $1,500,000 of adjusted basis (row 7 is the rule; the division is arithmetic).
7. **The 30-day window is a filing window, not a tax-payment window** (row 16). The election is filed with the Internal Revenue office where the person files a return, with a copy to the employer (row 17). The draft may say the election may be filed before the transfer — the regulation does.
8. **The holding-period sentence is the mechanism** (row 18), and it is the only place the draft may claim that an election moves the clock: without it the clock starts at vesting, with it at transfer. Do not extend that to a claim about when income is recognised for any other instrument.
9. **No claim about restricted stock units.** Nothing in this verified set states when an RSU's holding period starts. The draft may say only that an unvested option is not stock until it is exercised, which rows 10 and 16 support.
