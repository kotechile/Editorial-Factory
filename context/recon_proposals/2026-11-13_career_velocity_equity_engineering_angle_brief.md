**Emergent Collision Point:** Neither document prices the other's number. Carta measures the mark — the price per share a round agrees to, which is the number every offer letter, refresh and grant valuation is quoted against. Forge measures the clearing price — what a holder who is allowed to sell actually gets. In Q3 the mark went to a record at most stages while the clearing price went *below* it: the median trade moved from a 3% discount in August to a 9% in September, and the top of the distribution lost more than the median did, with the 90th-percentile premium collapsing from 58% to 15%. Carta adds the reason the two can drift without anyone lying: the record was set by a fifth fewer deals on rounds that have more than doubled in average size, so the mark rests on a thinner set of transactions than at any point since 2023. The story is the gap — a grant valued at the round's price is now valued above the only price the market is clearing at, and the premium that used to sit on the best names evaporated four times faster than the median.

**Hook:** Carta reported that median startup valuations are higher than their 2021 peak at most stages — in the same quarter Forge's own trading data shows the median private share changing hands at 9% below the last round's price, up from a 3% discount a month earlier.

**Tension:** Who this empowers: the candidate or employee who prices an equity offer at the secondary clearing level rather than the round's headline, and who asks what the last round's price per share was, when it was set, and whether anyone has cleared shares since. Who this threatens: the recruiter selling a grant at a record mark into a market where the top-decile premium fell 43 points in one month, and the holder who treats a tender price as a floor. The systemic friction: the record marks are being set by fewer, larger rounds concentrated in one sector, so the median company's valuation is increasingly a reading of somebody else's market.

**Target reader:** equity_career_strategist (senior IC to VP-level tech professional)

**Single claim to defend:** In Q3 2026 the primary-round mark and the secondary clearing price decoupled — median clearing fell from a 3% premium-adjacent discount to 9% below the last round, the top-decile premium collapsed from 58% to 15%, and the record marks themselves were set by 21% fewer deals — so an equity grant is worth what a holder could clear it for, not what the last round priced it at.
