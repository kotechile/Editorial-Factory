## Gate rulings for the drafter

1. **The $599 is not "any Series 12".** Write it as the figure Apple's estimator returned for a Series 12 with GPS and Cellular, as iFixit read it on 6 October, and state that the same failure is priced differently by case: MacRumors read a cellular aluminum model at $349 and the ceramic case at $599. Never write "Apple charges $599 to fix a watch" without the configuration.
2. **"No parts" means Apple sells none.** iFixit itself sells Watch batteries for older models, so the claim is about the manufacturer's parts channel, not about the existence of a battery anywhere. Never write "you cannot buy a Watch battery".
3. **Memory prices do not explain watch prices.** TrendForce's figures cover DRAM and NAND contract prices and notebook bill-of-materials costs. The watch's repair and retail prices are set by Apple's own list. Keep the two price lists separate in every sentence.
4. **No derived percentages.** $349 against $399 is 87.5% and $599 against $899 is 66.6%; the article may state the two dollar figures side by side, but no ratio, payback period or percentage-of-price figure is authorised anywhere in the body, the numbers bullets or the headline.
5. **The compliance claim is a belief, not a ruling.** "iFixit says it believes" — never "ten states have ruled".
6. **Do not report the Minnesota attorney-general exchange as established fact.** It rests on iFixit's post and a public forum thread; if used, write "iFixit reports" and keep it to one clause, or leave it out.
7. **Battery capacity figures are engineering detail, not savings.** 1.461 Wh and 2.444 Wh may appear once each at most; they are not part of the argument.
