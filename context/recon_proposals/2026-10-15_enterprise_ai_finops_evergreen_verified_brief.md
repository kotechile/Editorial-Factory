## 2. FLAGs and scope notes (carried into the draft, not hidden)

- **Scope note — the inversion is a measured pair of distilled open models, not a law about any vendor's tiers.** OckBench's 57% figure prices DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B against the 14B sibling on a third-party gateway, with the pricing basis dated January 2026 [2]. The draft carries it as the measured demonstration of the mechanic and never as a claim that a specific commercial tier is dearer than its larger sibling.
- **Scope note — Terminal-Bench burn is per agent scaffold, over the suite's own trials, not per user task.** The 137.5M input-token figure belongs to GPT-5.2 running inside the Codex CLI scaffold; a different scaffold on the same model burns a different amount. The draft attributes the number to the scaffold and the suite, never to the model alone.
- **Provenance labels are part of the claim.** Rows 1–5 are measured (two peer-reviewed-style benchmark papers); rows 6–9 are the vendors' own rate cards and API documentation and are labelled as vendor claims everywhere they appear.
- **Two dated step-ups are cited deliberately.** Google's Gemini 3.7 / 3.8 Flash introductory pricing ends 2026-12-31 and Gemini 4 Argon's promotional rate ends 2026-11-30 (standard from 2026-12-01) [7]. These are the "as of" anchors for the piece: the method does not expire, the price sheet does.
- **No review-labour figure is carried.** The 2026-10-08 evergreen run dropped a CRW-plus-human-review candidate because no fetchable primary carries an enterprise reviewer-minute or review-rate figure; that gap still holds, so this piece prices the *machine* term only and says so where it matters.
