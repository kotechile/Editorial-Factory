**Claims removed:** none. **Claims flagged:**
- **The CCA generation price is not on the page.** SDG&E's tables show delivery charges only for CCA customers and state explicitly that "CCA customers must also pay for electric generation at prices determined by the CCA … For CCA electric generation prices, please contact your CCA." No verbatim figure exists for a CCA generation rate, so the article may say the *delivery half* of a CCA bill is flat on these plans and that the generation half is billed separately at a price the utility does not publish — it may **not** claim a CCA customer's total bill has no spread.
- **Both utility pages exclude the fixed charges.** SDG&E's prices exclude a residential Base Services Charge effective October 2025; Con Edison's exclude various surcharges and add a $21.00 monthly customer charge. Any comparison must say the arbitrage is measured against the energy prices only.
- **"Once a day" is an assumption, not a sourced figure.** No cited page states a cycling frequency; the article must present the daily cycle as the reader's own assumption and state that more cycles earn more (bounded by the household's peak-period load).
- **Tier and baseline caveats.** SDG&E's TOU-DR1/TOU-DR2 and Standard DR rows are baseline-tiered (Tier 1 up to 130% of baseline); TOU-ELEC and EV-TOU-5 print a single price row per period. Quote the single-row plans for clean arithmetic and say so.
- **No installed price, no incentive.** The federal 25C credit expired December 31, 2025 (recorded in the 2026-10-12 home_equity_tco verified brief); no battery price could be fetched from a primary page. Every payback in the article must be stated per $1,000 of installed cost, or against a price the reader supplies from their own quote.
**Note for the drafter:** the load-bearing insight is structural, not a price forecast — delivery and generation are two different bills, and the arbitrage the hardware earns is the gap the reader's own two prices publish. Keep every figure attached to its plan name and its effective date; the numbers move, the check does not.
