## Gate rulings for the drafter

1. **Never write "Bitwarden went closed source".** The vendor's own edit says "Nothing has been made closed source", and the clients repository still defaults to GPLv3 (rows 6, 8). The accurate sentence is that the store build is now the commercial build.
2. **No price claim of any kind.** No price, no tier change and no subscription figure was announced. Any dollar figure in this piece would be invention.
3. **Do not say self-hosting broke.** Bitwarden says self-hosting is unchanged (row 4), and that is what the documents support. Vaultwarden's maintainer is *uncertain*, not contradicted — quote him with the uncertainty intact (row 9), never as confirmation that users are unaffected.
4. **"Commercial Modules" is the licence's term of art** (row 7). Gloss it on first use as the parts of the code covered by the Bitwarden License rather than GPLv3, or the sentence that follows makes no sense.
5. **The non-production clause is about Commercial Modules, not about the whole app.** Write it that way; the claim that the entire free app is "licensed for testing only" is stronger than the document supports, because the GPLv3 build carries no such clause.
6. **Row 13 is attributive only.** If the audit appears at all it appears as what an audit claims, with no count, size or route figure attached. The desk did not reproduce the artifact diff.
7. **The notice's date (9 October) is not printed on the page the desk can read** — community.bitwarden.com answers 403 to a non-browser client. Date it from the thread and the coverage, and do not build an argument on the exact day.
8. **The 60-day renewal window belongs to a paid self-hosted organisation's licence file** (row 11), not to a free individual install. Do not put it in a sentence about free users.
