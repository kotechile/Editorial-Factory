## Gate rulings for the drafter

1. **Do not compare 18x to 18.0 TB/s as if one were bigger.** They measure different things against different bases. The article's whole claim is that the comparison is invalid; the numbers must appear with their bases attached.
2. **Neither part is buyable this quarter.** Maia 300 is 2027 and Microsoft's own; AI250 samples in the first half of 2027. Never write as though either can be ordered.
3. **Call the Qualcomm figures estimates**, not measurements — B5's own footnote says normalised to target operating conditions. Say "Qualcomm's estimate" at least once.
4. **The 40% tokens-per-megawatt baseline is unnamed** — never attach it to a named competitor.
5. **Nvidia material is dated background** (announcement 2026-08-26). Use it in one paragraph, no more, and never as an anchor.
6. **Excluded prior figure:** do not reuse the SK hynix operating margin or the DRAM inventory-day figures from the 2026-10-28 memory-capacity piece; this article is about measurement bases, not supply.
7. Acronyms to expand at first use: HBM, HBM4, LPDDR, TDP, JEDEC.
8. The 18x and 5x figures live in the trade interview, not in Qualcomm's own post; cite both rows where each figure appears.
