## Drafter instructions (from the extraction, not the draft)

1. **Two scales, never one ratio.** 91.4%–99.7% is root-cause **service** accuracy (did it name the right service); 36.8%–60.3% is **recovery validity** (is the predicted plan inside the incident's valid action set). Do not write them as accuracy-and-recall, and do not divide one by the other.
2. **Table VII's base is invalid plans (755), not incidents.** 67.9% of invalid plans chose the wrong operation; only 1.3% missed the target service or dependency. That ordering is the article's spine.
3. **Fault class decides recovery, and the split is extreme:** DNS faults 0 of 48 in live replay against pod failure 36 of 36 and CPU saturation 36 of 36. Any statement that "agents fix what they find" must carry that split.
4. **Verified recovery is a harness event, not an agent claim.** SWEOps requires a declared recovery *and* passage of fault-specific functional checks; the paper counts unattempted or failed checks as unsuccessful. The agent's "fixed" is not evidence.
5. **Do not merge corpora.** 302 incidents on Online Boutique (R2Act) and 55 historical defects across 16 operator projects (SWEOps-Bench) are two populations measured by two harnesses; they agree on the mechanism.
6. **Vendor numbers are contrast only.** 94% RCA accuracy is a claim about naming a cause; neither benchmark measures that as the outcome that matters.
7. **Dates:** R2Act v1 = 6 July 2026; SWEOps-Bench v1 = 4 November 2026. Any restatement of the figures carries its vintage in the sentence.
