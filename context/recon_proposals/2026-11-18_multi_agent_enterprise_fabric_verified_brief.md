## Binding rulings for the drafter

1. **Both anchors are one controlled harness each, not field rates.** Anchor 1 measures a single dataset-fetching task (`data_fetch_and_store`) in a synthetic MCP environment; the paper says its limits "affect the generality of rates, not the core finding". Never write "100% of enterprise agents" — write "100% of 50 trials" and name the client.
2. **1.00 is three of five clients, never five.** Claude Sonnet 5's direct violation in C10 is 0.06 and Gemini's is 0.84. The honest sentence is "three of five clients sent every one of 50 trials to the impostor".
3. **Clarification is not a pass.** Claude's 0.94 clarification rate in C10 comes with a binding-stability score of 0.00 — the client never resolved a provider at all. Do not credit it as a defence; the paper's own line is that caution "does not create provider-bound tool identity".
4. **Never merge the two violation regimes.** C10 (copied description) and C12 (authority-enhanced description) are separate conditions with separate tables; 0.96 and 0.26 are Claude's C12 numbers, not C10.
5. **Experience Rot's headline pair is 0.23 against 0.63, not 0.23 against 1.00.** The 1.00 is valid experience — the same agent with a memory that still matches the service. Quoting 0.23 against 1.00 overstates the novice comparison; the novice number is 0.63.
6. **The 0.23 row is the incomplete-documentation case (four changes, "meaning of a number").** With complete documentation and a field-name change the out-of-date figure is 0.26; do not present 0.23 as a universal rate.
7. **"Reports the task as done" is the share of failed runs whose final reply claims success** (0.92–1.00 across conditions), not a share of all runs. Write it as a share of failures.
8. **The memory-form numbers are one column of Table 5 under five changes and six models.** The conversation-history form (0.47) is not comparable to the memory-file form (0.72) without saying which form the fabric uses.
9. **Metabase is friction, not an anchor.** It is a design retrospective with no measurement, so it may corroborate what the industry ships but no argument may rest on it, and its 10-of-24 result is a tool-count design choice, not evidence about correctness.
10. **Desk arithmetic that must be shown as arithmetic:** 0.63 − 0.23 = 0.40 (the 40-point gap below novices) and 1.00 − 0.23 = 0.77 (the cost column's own value). Both are the paper's subtraction, not a new measurement.
11. **Not cited, deliberately:** guardscope (arXiv:2611.15223), SECCARD (arXiv:2611.18887), OfficePrism (arXiv:2611.22262), Reversibility Classes (arXiv:2611.22075) and the code-mode study (arXiv:2611.19123) — each was scored and refused in the angle brief. Do not cite them as corroboration; `--check-briefs` would also require their URLs in the signals file.
12. **Do not say the attacker "hijacks the model".** The paper's framing is a selection failure produced by unbound metadata; the model is not compromised and no data left the sandbox in any trial ("No experiment uses real user data, exfiltrates data, or produces external side effects.").
