## Notes for the fact-check pass
- R2Act's headline pair is a *service-level* accuracy against a *plan-level* validity, not two scores on one scale: 91.4%–99.7% is root-cause **service** accuracy; 36.8%–60.3% is **recovery validity**. Do not write them as "RCA accuracy 91.4% and recall 36.8%".
- Table VII's denominator is 755 invalid plans (513 wrong operation + 224 invalid structure + 8 no-op + 10 wrong target), so 67.9% and 29.7% and 1.3% are shares of *invalid plans*, not shares of all incidents. Keep the noun attached.
- SWEOps-Bench's 14.5%–52.7% is **verified recovery** (the harness re-checks the fault), not "the agent said it fixed it"; the paper's own distinction is that "clearing the runtime symptom can still fail fix-specific functional verification" and that some tasks recover with an *incorrect* reported root cause. Both directions are load-bearing: an RCA score is not a proxy for usefulness in either direction.
- 146 of 302 in R2Act's validity-gated live replay is the third number, not a fourth: offline-valid predictions did not all recover live.
- Do not merge the two benchmarks' populations (302 K8s incidents on Online Boutique; 55 tasks across 16 operator projects). Two corpora, one mechanism.
