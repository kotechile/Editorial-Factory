## Rules for the drafter

1. Say "whichever is higher", never "whichever is lower", for an undertaking (row 1–3, 9). The SME
   carve-out (row 4) is the only place where the lower figure binds, and it applies to SMEs only.
2. The 7 % band attaches to Article 5 prohibited practices **only** (row 1). Do not describe the 7 %
   figure as the general AI Act penalty for non-compliance with high-risk obligations — that is the
   3 % band (row 2).
3. Deployer duties under Article 26 are named in the 3 % band's own list (row 2). Human oversight,
   monitoring and six-month log retention are Article 26 duties (row 11), so they are 3 % exposure.
4. The high-risk obligations themselves begin 2 December 2027 / 2 August 2028 (row 7) while the
   penalty machine has been running since 2 August 2025 (rows 5, 6). Both dates belong in the piece.
5. GDPRs 4 % band runs alongside, not instead (rows 9, 10). Do not add the bands together — the
   article may say the same conduct is read under both, never that the percentages sum.
6. Never state a national authority's practice or a decided case: none was verified here.
7. All derived figures (row 13) must be recomputed from rows 1–3 and 9 after any rewrite.
