## Gate rulings for the drafter (each is load-bearing)
1. **USMCA has not lapsed and no tariff changed on October 2.** Say the review is now annual and the comment docket is open; never write "USMCA ended", "preferences are gone", or "duties were cut".
2. **January 12, 2027 is a comment-and-hearing-request deadline, not a decision date.** No source states when the 2027 review concludes.
3. **$21 billion is a ceiling ("up to"), not a commitment.** Say "up to $21 billion". The first ~$10 billion funds fab one; the second ~$10 billion "would fund" fab two — conditional mood.
4. **The four percent equity is a separate agreement from the fab funding.** Attribute it as such; do not present it as the price of the fabs.
5. **First output is 2029 at the earliest, for fab one.** Do not imply capacity is available now; do not give the second fab a date (the source gives none).
6. **900,000 wafers is annual capacity per fab when fully operational**, in 300mm wafers. Never convert it to chips, revenue or dollars — no such figure exists.
7. **Japan is the funder; GlobalFoundries is the operator and technology provider.** Do not write that the U.S. government is funding the fabs.
8. **GLM Components:** the suspension covers "unliquidated entries of goods from the GLM Components facility" — not all Mexican auto parts, not the company's other plants, not already-liquidated entries. The 10-day/45-day clocks are for Mexico's review, not for a U.S. determination.
9. **Do not restate the Omdia memory figure as a primary.** If used at all: "Supply Chain Dive, citing Omdia research, reports memory prices up to 400% higher over two years" — reporter's attribution, no primary report URL was retrievable.
10. **No arithmetic the sources do not state.** Do not compute a percentage of capacity, a cost per wafer, a share of U.S. demand, or a dollar impact per container.
