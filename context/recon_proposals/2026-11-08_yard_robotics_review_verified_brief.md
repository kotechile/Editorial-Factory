## Rulings for the drafter

1. Every runtime, coverage and slope number in the piece is the **manufacturer's own claim**, except the
   Gadgeteer's 200 minutes, which is a reviewer restating the maker's number. Label them; the desk measured
   nothing.
2. Never compute a dock-to-dock time for the LUBA — its full-charge time is unpublished (row 16). Cycles,
   yes; minutes, no.
3. Do not call either machine antenna-free. Both depend on a network correction service; the X350 ships the
   antenna in the box, and the LUBA's 4G service is free for three years with an unpriced renewal.
4. The 0.75-acre reader gets the Kress paragraph, with both of the maker's own prices and the dealer-only
   caveat. Do not present $1,499.99 as a price anyone can click.
5. No first-person claim that a unit was run, measured or tried; the honesty paragraph says what the desk
   worked from and that it handled neither machine.
