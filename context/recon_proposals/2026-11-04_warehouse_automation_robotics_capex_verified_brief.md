## Rulings for the drafter (do not cross)

1. **"Initial commitment" is the source's own hedge.** The agreement is "an initial commitment of approximately $6 billion in spend" — not a purchase order, not capex booked, not a contract value. Never write "Walmart will spend $6 billion" as a completed fact; write the commitment as committed.
2. **Do not divide the $6 billion by 25, or by any unit count, and present it as a price.** The release states one aggregate figure and no per-site breakdown; a per-building average would be arithmetic on an assumption about even spread that the source never makes. The article may say the release publishes no per-building price. (This is the row the desk removed for Tractor Supply on 2026-10-14 and the same rule applies.)
3. **A3's index is not a warehouse automation index.** Its scope excludes AMRs and humanoids. Never say "warehouse robot orders rose 24.8%" — say the industrial-robot order index rose 24.8% and note what it excludes.
4. **$65,800 is a derived average of ordered industrial robots**, not a warehouse robot price and not Symbotic's price. Label it as the quarter's order value divided by its units.
5. **The 25 sites are not new.** They sit inside the 42-building programme announced in 2022. The release does not say the 2022 retrofit is finished, and the article must not assert that it is or is not — say the plan runs eight years from 2022 and the layer lands inside it.
6. **Walmart's own words claim outcomes, not numbers** — "improve service, grow capacity, simplify work for our associates and lower costs". Quote it as its own claim; do not attach a figure to it.
7. **No payback, IRR or depreciation term may be computed for this deal.** None is published. The article's point is precisely that the unit does not exist in either release.
8. **The cellumation acquisition is out of scope for this piece** — `2026-10-21_your-seller-wont-stay` already reported it (see the angle brief's §3.5 note). Do not cite it in the body.
9. The Louisiana figure ($330 million-plus for one regional DC) is a *different* project by the same buyer, reported by trade press; if used at all it must be attributed as separate and not blended into the $6 billion.
