## Notes for the drafter
- All three figures on cognitops are the vendor's own cost model citing BLS JOLTS / Center for American Progress — cite them as the breakdown host's estimates, and attribute the 40%-a-year separation rate to BLS JOLTS as the page itself does.
- The Gallup 40%-of-salary rule (row 1) and the Center for American Progress 20%-of-pay figure (row 4) are different bases applied to different bands — never merge them into one number. The Fountain $21,800 applies Gallup's 40% to warehousing's $54,500 average; the CAP 20% applies to under-$50,000 earners. Present as a band, never an average.
- The wage anchor ($19.35, 2025 vintage, O*NET) is lower than the April 2026 earnings series ($26.76) Fountain cites — different series (occupation median vs industry average). Use each only for what it is.
- Derived arithmetic the article may carry (its own math, not in any source): 200-person DC × 40% separations = 80 exits/year; 80 × $19,000 (20% of $40,240 O*NET median... actually of an annualized wage near $47k) — keep any derived total's inputs visible in the sentence.
