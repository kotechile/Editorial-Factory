## 2. FLAGs and scope notes (carried into the draft, not hidden)

- **FLAG A — two official LangChain pages state different numbers for "the" default.** `docs.langchain.com` (LangGraph Graph API) documents a graph-level default of **1000** super-steps since 1.0.6; `reference.langchain.com` (langchain_core `RunnableConfig.recursion_limit`) documents **25**. They describe different layers (graph run vs. call recursion) and different package versions, so this is not a contradiction to resolve but a fact the reader has to hold: **the number an agent inherits depends on which layer sets it.** The draft states both numbers, attributes each to its page, and does not claim one supersedes the other.
- **FLAG B — the vendor's own tracker puts a third number on the subagent path.** Issue #1698 in `langchain-ai/deepagents` reports subagents running at the framework default of 25 regardless of the parent's 150/300, and describes the failure as silent (an error raised for the subagent, not the parent). The issue is closed and references a follow-up bug number; the draft reports what the tracker says and dates it, and does not claim the current release still behaves that way.
- **Provenance labels are part of the claim.** Rows 1–8 are measured (a static-analysis study with a manual-review precision number); rows 9–12 are vendor documentation or a vendor tracker and are labelled as such everywhere they appear.
- **Scope note — the census is not a claim about any specific framework's default.** The paper counts confirmed failures in real repositories by framework; it does not attribute the failures to the frameworks' own defaults. The draft keeps the census and the documented defaults as two separate legs and states the join as the article's own reading.
- **The desk field note is not a gate row.** `context/growth_os/customer-truth.md` §`agentic_ai` Anecdote 1 (an autonomous multi-agent loop that burned $4,200 in API credits in 45 minutes before rate limits stopped it; the recorded lesson is deterministic recursion budgets — max steps = 5 — plus per-session cost ceilings). It is carried as a labelled desk field note in `## Sources`, never as a cited row, exactly as the 2026-10-10/10-14 runs did with their field notes.
- **No dollar figure is derived for the reader.** No cited source prices an agent loop, so the article prints none: the $4,200 number is the desk's own anonymised field case, labelled as a field case.
