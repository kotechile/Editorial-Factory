## Gate rules for the drafter

- **Both legs clear the dual-anchor gate**: leg A has A1–A5 VERIFIED, leg B has B1–B3 VERIFIED. Two REMOVED: none.
- **Do not print a SmartThings price.** The announcement publishes no rate. Say "a rate SmartThings has not published" — it is the sharpest fact in the piece and it is true.
- **Do not say the washer's integration costs 100,000 calls per washer.** The quota is per developer account, shared across up to 10 OAuth apps. The headline compresses; the body must state the per-account basis inside the first two sections.
- **Do not claim AUX-2 rescues appliances.** R2 bounds it: audio, serial, Bluetooth, radio stick, Grove. The appliance path is LocalThings or buying differently.
- **Do not describe the paid plan as affecting SmartThings' own app or local Edge drivers.** A1: both stay free. The toll is on the third-party cloud path only.
- **A4 is the defect, not the price.** The country clause is the single most arguable fact in the piece; quote it.
- Derived arithmetic (B4) is ours. Label it, keep it to one sentence, and do not convert it into "your house will go over the quota".
- Dates: cutoff **2026-12-15**, new integration ships in the **2026-12-02** release, plans launched **2026-11-04**, AUX-2 launched **2026-11-11**, $69 / €59. State the timeline once in paragraph 2.
