## Rulings for the drafter
1. Walmart's fiscal quarter is **Q3 FY27**, ended **October 31, 2026**, reported **November 19, 2026**. Never print "Q3 2027" as a calendar quarter.
2. The "almost 3x" is sub-30-minute *deliveries* in Q3, per Guggina's own words — never generalize to "deliveries tripled".
3. The 15-minute pick is an **assumption inside** the 30-minute promise ("mind you, that assumes…") — the network coverage claim (60% of households) depends on it. Do not present the pick time as a separate disclosed metric with its own KPI.
4. The 23% is **US Walmart e-commerce**; the 17% is **global**. Never blend, never attribute 23% to the consolidated company.
5. The >80% store-fulfilled share covers **e-commerce orders including marketplace orders** — not all orders, not all units.
6. Target's OSA gain is **a full percentage point YoY**, from Target's own summary page; the "more than a full point" phrasing belongs to COO Roath via Supply Chain Dive (corroboration row 9). Prefer the primary page's plain "a full percentage point".
7. Target's GM growth is 2.3 ppt **including** 1.1 ppt of tariff refunds — never present the margin gain as operating improvement alone.
8. No hands-on claim: this pipeline has no store data. Every figure is the retailers' own disclosure.
9. Claims 11–12 are the desk's derived bridge and must be argued, not quoted.
10. The 96% (three hours) / 60% (30 minutes) coverage figures are Walmart's own network statements on the call — carry the "households" base every time.
