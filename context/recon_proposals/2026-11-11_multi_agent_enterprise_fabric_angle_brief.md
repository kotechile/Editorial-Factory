# Angle Brief: multi_agent_enterprise_fabric — 2026-11-11
**Angle Type:** Single-Signal
**Winner:** Your Workflow Outlives Your Model (the workflow is a portable artifact with its own quality, and a model swap silently rewrites it)
**Scores:** N=9.0 A=9.5 S=9.0 → Composite=9.2
**Anchor:** WorkflowArena, Jiang, Niu, Peng et al. (Navers Lab / Einsia.AI / Tsinghua University), submitted 2026-11-09 — https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12856 . 145 long-horizon tasks across 8 domains and 17 task forms; 22 model–harness–effort configurations; every delegation recorded; body figures read from the HTML record (https://arxiv.org/html/2611.12856v1).

**Hook:** A workflow one model writes and another model runs is worth 6.4 points. The benchmark measured that on 32 shared tasks: Opus-authored workflows lifted every executor — Kimi from 35.75 to 46.61, DeepSeek from 18.37 to 25.24 — averaging +6.41 points in static mode and +6.27 in dynamic mode, while workflows authored by Astra, a system that scores 30.80% on the same benchmark, averaged −2.53 points in static mode. The author never touched the workspace, so the difference is the workflow and the instructions it carries into each worker.

**Tension:** The fabric market sells the opposite unit of change. SAP's TechEd 2026 pitch is one governed platform with "more than 70 AI models" behind a governed pipeline, plus an Enterprise AI Gateway that "will consolidate authentication, policy enforcement, routing, monitoring, usage tracking and auditing" (https://news.sap.com/?p=248010). If the model that authors the workflow sets its shape and its transfer value, then the model menu is not a menu of interchangeable engines: it is a menu of topologies, and every model or effort change re-authors the thing the change board approved. On the same benchmark, effort alone moves Astra's workflow calls per task by 67% (1.33 → 2.22) while its group size moves 5%; Opus and Sonnet delegate 0.86 and 0.76 times per task in groups of 5.55 and 6.14 workers, while Astra and Sol call 2.09 and 1.94 times in groups of 1.72 and 1.69.

**Target reader:** ai_architect
**Single claim to defend:** In an agent fabric the orchestration is a separable, measurable artifact — pin down who authors the workflow, version that workflow, and benchmark it on your own tasks, because model identity and effort level change the topology you approved, and workflow quality does not follow from task performance.
