**Notes, divergences and gate rulings for the drafter**
- **`+7.5% / >73%` is one Census figure reported twice** (ABC 2026-10-01 and Eye On Housing 2026-10-01, both from the same Census release). Cite it once, attributed to the Census data as analysed by ABC (or to Eye On Housing); never present it as two independent measurements.
- **"A full three months more" is the source's own framing.** Do not convert it into "8.3 months" for the contractors without data-center work — that is a derived difference no source states.
- **The collision is about time, not price.** NAHB says competition from data-center construction is "extending the time it takes to complete projects". No source claims data centers raise a homeowner's price. ABC names materials, labor and borrowing costs — separately, and for commercial contractors. Keep those two mechanisms apart.
- **Sentiment is not volume.** The RMI is a diffusion index of remodeler opinion; it says nothing about dollars. Row 12 is the dollar series and it is negative year over year. Do not write "remodeling is booming", "demand is strong", or "spending is rising" without the −7.4% in the same breath.
- **Both legs are trade-association member surveys** (ABC's own members, NAHB's remodeler members), not independent measurement. Say so once, in plain words, in the catch.
- **Do not infer typical contract terms.** No source establishes how common completion dates or crew-continuity clauses are in residential remodeling contracts. The tactical section reports what the indices show and what to watch; it must not assert standard practice.
- **No wage figures.** Electrician pay, per-trade rates and "shortage of X thousand workers" all come from recruiter content and vendor blogs, not primaries in this window. Excluded.
