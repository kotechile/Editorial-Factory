## Ruled out (no drafting permitted on these)

- **A service-life figure** ("a central AC lasts 7 to 15 years", anyone's chart). The only fetchable reference is InterNACHI's life-expectancy table, and every integer in its HVAC block (`7`, `15`, `15 to 25`, `60 to 100`) collides with an unrelated earlier row on the same page, so a gate row's evidence window would read as a coincidence. The draft therefore states no expected service life and never implies a payback period. This is the reason the "repair or replace: annualised break-even" candidate was dropped (same reason the 2026-10-12 run dropped it).
- **Any equipment, duct-sealing or diagnostic price, and any incentive dollar figure.** The federal 25C credits expired 2025-12-31; no price in this table means no payback arithmetic.
- **Any claim that duct sealing *saves* a stated percentage.** The page states the leakage penalty (15-20%), not the measured saving from a retrofit; the only retrofit claim it adds is qualitative (reduced on-times after a duct retrofit), which the draft may report as qualitative only.
- **Any SEER-to-SEER2 conversion or efficiency-percentage claim.** The regulation states the floor per product class; the test procedures differ, so no conversion is made.
