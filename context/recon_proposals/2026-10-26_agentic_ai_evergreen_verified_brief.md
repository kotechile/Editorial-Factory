## Rules for the drafter

- Segment's services were product services and Lenarduzzi's were business processes; neither is an MCP tool server. The transfer to an agent runtime's tool layer is the desk's reading and must be written as a reading, not as either source's claim.
- The 20% effort premium (claim 8) is load-bearing: without it the article becomes a microservices endorsement.
- The Anthropic figure is a vendor's measurement of its own pattern. Use it for the mechanism (each MCP server adds tool definitions to what must be discovered and loaded), never as a benchmark of the reader's fleet, and label it as the vendor's number.
- Do not state a "break-even service count"; no source measures one, and inventing one violates rule 3.
