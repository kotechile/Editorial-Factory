## Gate rulings for the drafter

1. **Two different growth figures, neither a rounding of the other.** A1's endpoints are 2,500 a day → over 30,000 a day; A6's "10x" is the source's own sentence about the volume of code merged per day during the five-month migration. Quote 10× only where that sentence is being used, and if the piece states the multiple implied by A1, it is the desk's arithmetic on two published endpoints (about twelve times) — say so, and never present twelve as the source's number.
2. **The deadlines are durations from 2026-10-09, not calendar dates.** The announcement says "another year" and "six months". April 2027 and October 2027 are arithmetic on the announcement date; if used at all, label them as approximate and anchored to 2026-10-09.
3. **Every percentage and multiple stays bound to its own unit.** 200× is CPU and memory footprint against the previous mirroring infrastructure. Two orders of magnitude is checkout speed, "in seconds rather than minutes". 25% is CI CPU capacity recovered. None of the three is a cost figure, a latency figure or an engineering-hours figure.
4. **"The Deno team is joining Cloudflare" is the documented event; no price, terms or deal value is published.** The piece must not describe an acquisition price or a valuation.
5. **The honesty clause is load-bearing, not a caveat at the end.** Anthropic's own text says Scion is not a replacement for git in general use, suits only truly enormous monorepos, and that every company rebuilding it alone would waste engineering effort (A8), and that its internal token budget is unlike other businesses (A9). A piece that reads as "build your own version control" contradicts the source it cites.
6. **celld was released in August 2026 and is being merged into workerd.** It is not a product of the acquisition, and no ship date for the merged self-hosting path exists beyond "in the coming months" (Cloudflare's words, no date).
7. **No hands-on claim and no measured field data.** The desk has not tested Deno Deploy, cloudflare Workers or Scion. Any desk field note from `customer-truth.md` may appear only as a labelled field note, not as a measurement of these vendors.
