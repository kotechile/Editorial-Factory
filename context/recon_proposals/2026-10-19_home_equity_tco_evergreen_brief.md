**Thesis:** The assessment cap limits the drift, never the addition — in every capped state the prior year's taxable value is multiplied by the cap and the new construction is added on top at full market value — so a permitted addition is a permanent carrying-cost step the payback model omits, it lands on the roll the first January 1 after the work is substantially completed, and the mechanical renewals the same statutes do not classify as new construction are the cheaper capital on both sides of the ledger.
