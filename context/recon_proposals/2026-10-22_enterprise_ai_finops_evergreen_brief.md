**Decision the reader is facing:** Whether to keep a human reviewer on every AI output — and, if so, which pay grade reviews it — because the review minutes, not the tokens, dominate the per-unit bill, so the automation's business case is settled by the reviewer's wage and the minutes they spend rather than by the model's price.
**Durability:** The arithmetic is structural and survives every rate-card change: a review minute costs the reviewer's hourly wage divided by sixty, and the review ceiling is the manual cost per unit minus the model cost per unit, divided by that per-minute price — so the same review is a different business case at a different pay grade.
