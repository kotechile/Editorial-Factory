## FLAGGED (carry the caveat into the draft)
- **F1 — the measured leg is requirements classification, not agent evals.** Four AI4RE tasks with small real datasets. The transfer to a team's own agent suite is an argument, never a measurement about the reader's harness. Say so where the numbers are introduced.
- **F2 — 6–43.8 points is a range across four tasks, not a typical lift.** Quote the concrete pair (security task, 0.325 → 0.763 = +43.8) whenever a single number is needed, and never generalise it to "your F1 will double".
- **F3 — twenty was the study's arm, never a tuned value.** The paper compares `samples_per_prompt` 1 against 20; it does not optimise that integer. Present 20 as the measured arm, not as a recommended setting.
- **F4 — the curation drop depends on an unoptimised threshold.** The authors call the 20% removal "a pragmatic choice … rather than an empirically optimized value", so the 9-of-12 result is a warning that a similarity filter can hurt, not a law that every dedupe hurts.
- **F5 — "not a duplicate detector" is my gloss.** The authors present duplicate-stability as robustness. Write it as: the score answers *how varied are these ideas*, and it is stable under copies, so it is not the number that tells you how many independent cases the gate runs. Never write "the metric is broken".
- **F6 — Cosmopedia's figures are first-party.** Hugging Face's own account of its own corpus; the "<1% duplicate" line is a vendor claim, and the draft must label it that way.
- **F7 — no hands-on claim.** This desk ran no generator, no corpus and no dedupe pass; nothing in the article may say "we generated", "we tested" or "our suite".
