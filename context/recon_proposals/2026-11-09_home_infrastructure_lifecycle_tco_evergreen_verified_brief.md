## Drafter's constraints

- **Legal, not banned.** The SNAP table lists R-410A as **Acceptable**. The ceiling restrains *new*
  equipment; recharging an existing R-410A system stays legal. Never write "banned" of the gas itself.
- **The ratio is derived arithmetic** (2,090 ÷ 700 = 2.99 → "three times"), and a derived figure is
  never quoted as a source's sentence.
- **No price anywhere.** No fetchable primary publishes what a pound of R-410A or an installed
  replacement costs; the article carries no dollar figure and says why that number is missing.
- **Percentages:** 90 / 60 / 30 / 20 / 15 percent come from the statute's own table and must not be
  restated as cumulative cuts ("a 60 percent cut" is wrong; it is *60 percent of baseline allowed*).
- **R-454B (470) and HFC-32 (675)** are the two low-GWP substitutes on the SNAP page for this
  subsector; both are **A2L** (mildly flammable) per the same table. Do not present A2L as a hazard
  claim without a source — the table is the only basis for the class name.
- **The 2029 step is the sharp one:** 60 percent allowed in 2024–2028, 30 percent in 2029–2033 — the
  allowance halves at that date.
- **Attribution:** EPA (restrictions table, SNAP listing, rule summary) and 42 U.S.C. 7675 (Cornell
  LII). The eCFR text is quoted as the codified rule, banner date 11/05/2026.
