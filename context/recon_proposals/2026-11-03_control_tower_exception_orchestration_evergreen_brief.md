**Thesis:** A temperature excursion is settled by a cumulative thermal budget, not by a peak reading — three readings of 5°C, 8°C and 14°C average 9.0°C arithmetically but compute to a mean kinetic temperature of 9.85°C under the USP <1079.2> formula, a controlled-room-temperature product may not exceed 40°C for more than 24 hours provided its MKT stays at or below 25°C, and a two-hour logging interval can miss a 45-minute excursion entirely — so the sample rate and the window are the architecture, and an alert rule that pages on the first setpoint breach is measuring the wrong variable.
