## Dating note (load-bearing for the durability claim)
The comparison paper is dated 26 Jul 2026 (`arXiv:2607.23884v1 [cs.MA] 26 Jul 2026`); the MCP revision that introduces MRTR is the 2026-07-28 version. The paper's R3 finding is therefore a pre-MRTR reading of the specification, and the article must say so in the same paragraph as the requirement score. What MRTR adds is an input round-trip inside a tool call; it does not add a task object, a subscription, an artifact stream or resumption.

## Arithmetic the article performs (derived, appears in no source — label it)
- 1,898 − 1,255 = 643 lines, the premium paid for the task version of one hop. (Source figures for both, subtraction is the article's.)
- 6 ÷ 2 = 3, the factor by which the task version's coordination stages exceed the tool call's per interaction.
- A handle reused across N calls implies N authorization checks, by the specification's own rule ("validate the caller's authorization against the handle on every call") — stated as a consequence, not as a measured count.
