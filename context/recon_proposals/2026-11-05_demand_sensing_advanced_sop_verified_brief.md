## Rulings the drafter must follow

1. **FLAGGED — the "47%" is respondents, not all supply chains.** Gartner's headline reads "47% of Supply Chains"; its own body says 303 leaders at organizations that had integrated AI "to some degree". Write "of 303 supply chain leaders surveyed", never "47% of supply chains". The population is AI-using organizations, so the figure describes adopters, not the industry.
2. **FLAGGED — do not total the 66% with the "other levels" share.** Gartner says entry-level concentration is "more than double the percentage reporting reduction at other levels"; the other-levels number is not printed. Never write "twice as many entry-level cuts as all other levels combined".
3. **FLAGGED — Kinaxis is one vendor, not the category.** Its 22% SaaS growth is one company's line, reported under IFRS, and it is a planning-and-orchestration vendor with a services arm. It is evidence that buyers are still signing, not a market growth rate; "the market grew 22%" is false.
4. **FLAGGED — the 2.1 years is a computed ratio, not a disclosed term.** RPO ÷ ARR is a standard proxy for average remaining contract length; Kinaxis does not disclose a weighted-average term. Label it computed from the two published figures and show the division.
5. **FLAGGED — the 70% effort figure is manual planning effort, not headcount.** DemandForecast.ai measures hours of review and cleansing in 15 of its own deployments (the engine in all 15 is Pecan's), so it is a vendor's own measurement: label it as such, and never convert the 70% into jobs. It is here because it shows what the review work *is*, not because it proves a cut.
6. **FLAGGED — the U.S. Bank 30% is shipper spend, not demand.** The index measures payments; spend can rise on rates and fuel while volume is flat, which is exactly what the write-up says. Use it as cost pressure, never as a demand signal.
7. **FLAGGED — dates.** The Gartner fieldwork ran 5 May to 11 June 2026 and was published 28 October 2026; say both. Kinaxis's quarter closed 30 September 2026 and the results were released 4 November 2026.
8. **Do not claim causation Gartner does not.** Gartner reports that respondents attributed the reduction "primarily" to AI; that is an attribution by respondents, not a measured causal effect. Write "the leaders surveyed attribute it to AI".
