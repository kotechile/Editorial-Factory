**Decision the reader is facing:** Whether to size a residency-bound inference service inside a confidential GPU enclave (CPU TEE + confidential GPU, SEV-SNP / TDX / H200-CC) on the vendor's quoted single-digit overhead or on the two- or three-digit overhead the small-batch measurements show — i.e. how much GPU capacity and tail-latency headroom to buy, and which measurement to demand from the platform team, before promising an SLA to a regulator-facing internal customer.
**Durability:** The overhead is a data-movement problem, not a model problem: confidential GPU mode encrypts every host-to-device and device-to-host transfer through a software bounce buffer, so the penalty lands on IO-bound traffic — batch-one decode, short inputs, multi-GPU collectives — and shrinks as batch and input length grow and compute starts to dominate. No model generation removes that path (TDX Connect and SEV-TIO are still in development), so the shape of the curve is stable even though every magnitude is dated: the H100 measurements are as of September 2024 and September 2025, the H100-under-TDX numbers as of May 2026, the H200 numbers as of June 2026, and the vendor's eight-B200 figures as of the 2026 Blackwell launch. A reader re-measures their own estate each hardware cycle; what they keep is the rule — budget headroom on the interactive path, price TTFT separately from throughput, and find the saturation knee before the business finds it.

**Thesis:** The overhead a confidential inference enclave costs is not a property of the enclave but of the workload shape it is asked to run — the same H100/TDX stack is quoted at under 5% by a vendor blog running an eight-GPU, framework-tuned, concurrency-16 batch and measured at 17.7% to 21.1% below baseline in global throughput by an independent single-GPU study, at 35.0% to 62.8% in tokens per second at small batch on an H200, and inside 4–8% by a tuning-heavy academic study whose own best case is large batch and long input — so the only defensible way to size a regulated inference estate is to reserve 15–25% capacity for the enclave's steady penalty, measure the saturation knee and the TTFT tail at the concurrency the workload actually runs, and refuse a vendor headline that was produced at a batch size the reader's agent traffic will never reach.
