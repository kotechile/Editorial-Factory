## Discrepancies and rulings for the drafter
1. **"From $0.99" vs "priced at $0.99".** The plan cards print a floor; the FAQ states the flat rate. Both are on the same page, so the article says "$0.99 a resolution as listed" and never "Intercom's price is $0.99 for every customer".
2. **Three vendors, four units, not one comparison.** Outcome (Intercom), automated resolution (Zendesk), conversation (Salesforce) and action credit (Salesforce) are different quantities. The article may only compare them after stating what each contains.
3. **The only legitimate human comparison is wage cost, not loaded cost.** $21.53 ÷ 60 = $0.3588 a minute → $1.44 at four minutes, $2.15 at six, $3.59 at ten. State these as wage-only figures with the handle time named as the reader's own input; never call them "cost per contact".
4. **Derived ratios, to be recomputed in the final from the endpoints above:** $500 ÷ 100,000 = $0.005 a credit, so 40 credits = $0.20; $0.99 ÷ $2.15 = 46%; $2.00 ÷ $2.15 = 93%; $0.20 ÷ $2.15 = 9%. None of these ratios is a source claim.
5. **Do not merge the Zendesk 80% claim with the Intercom 50% testimonial into a "real-world 50%" statistic.** They are different vendors, different samples and different definitions; the article may say an advertised ceiling and a reported outcome do not match, and may not average them.
6. **Sources count:** four vendor/agency hosts — intercom.com, zendesk.com, salesforce.com, onetonline.org — plus finops.org for the framing. All five fetched HTTP 200 on 2026-11-05.
