## Gate rulings for the drafter

1. **The pair is not slower than one box, and the article may not say it is.** The only measured clustered result in evidence is *up to* 1.7x a single 128GB system on Qwen3.8 27B (A4). Report that as the vendor's own number, unattributed to any hands-on test this desk ran.
2. **State the baseline honestly or not at all.** ServeTheHome-era coverage splits on whether the 1.7x baseline was a single 64GB unit or a single 128GB system (A4-note). The article may say "up to 1.7x, on a 27-billion-parameter test model", and if it names a baseline it must attribute it ("as StorageReview reads NVIDIA's numbers, a single 128GB system"). Do not present the baseline as settled.
3. **Never claim 546 GB/s across the link.** That figure is the pooled total reported by Spheron (B5) and equals two local 273 GB/s buses summed (B6). The link itself is 200 Gb/s, measured 196.08 (B1, B2), which the review-side source converts to about 25 GB/s (B3). Any sentence that puts 546 GB/s in the cable is a factual error.
4. **The weights-crossing-the-cable inference is the desk's reading, not a measurement.** It follows from B1–B3 and B6 (a 25 GB/s link against 273 GB/s of local memory) and must be written as arithmetic or labelled as the writer's reading — never as a benchmark result, and never as a claim that anyone measured a 200-billion-parameter model on a pair.
5. **Authorised arithmetic (the only derived numbers allowed):** $9,998 = 2 x $4,999 (B4); $3,048 = $9,998 - $6,950 (B4, A5); about 25 GB/s = 200 Gb/s ÷ 8 (B3); "about eleven times" = 273 ÷ 25 (B3, B6); "about three-quarters more" is *not* authorised — state the two prices instead.
6. **Do not re-argue the per-gigabyte inversion.** The $4,999-against-$6,950 price ladder and the "$78 against $54 a gigabyte" reading were published by this desk on 2026-10-13 (`your-32gb-card-gone`) and are a retread as a thesis. The prices appear here only as the cost of the pair. Do not add a per-gigabyte bullet.
7. **The $8,000 framing is a flagged alternate reading (B7).** It may appear once, in the catch, as NVIDIA's own rounding quoted by ServeTheHome — never as this desk's arithmetic, and never averaged with $9,998.
8. **No hands-on claim of any kind.** This desk has not run a two-unit cluster. "We tested", "our cluster", "in our lab" are barred; the reseller's measurement is cited as theirs (B2).
9. **Every acronym expanded at first use, and all-caps product tokens avoided.** Write "gigabytes (GB)" once; call the machine "the Spark" or "NVIDIA's desk box" rather than its three-letter family name; no bare all-caps part names, no bare "CUDA"-style stack tokens. The hardware partner list may be given in prose.
10. **Figures barred from the article entirely** (verified elsewhere but outside this thesis, and one of them is a retread anchor): the 748GB workstation's $85,000 list price, the 256GB Apple configuration at $11,299, and any rental-market median. If the piece needs a comparison to renting it must say plainly that no rental rate is being priced here.
