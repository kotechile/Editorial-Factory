## Cautions the drafter must carry

- **A5 is a limit, not a footnote.** Cisco's own team has not run Quarry on the July data; the reconstruction is a counterfactual. Never write that Quarry "would have caught" the Hugging Face intrusion — write that Cisco says it would have had points to flag, and that the test was not run.
- **The paper surveys six guardrails; do not name them as products that fail a test.** The claim is structural: none of the six implements cross-execution monitoring.
- **Kimi-K2.6's 3/30 is not relational detection.** The paper says its blocks "sit in the same band as its benign blocks" — single-step conservatism. Do not report it as a partial success.
- **20 of 60 vs 22 of 60 is the sentence the piece exists for**, and it is a failure to detect, not a failure to act: the guard allowed the run because no single action's stated purpose revealed the cumulative disclosure.
- **Derived arithmetic is allowed only if the numbers used are stated.** 22 of 60 = 37% (source states it); 20 of 60 = 33% (derived, may be written as "a third" or omitted).
