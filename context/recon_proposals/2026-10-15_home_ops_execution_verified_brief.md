## Notes for the drafter
- **Provenance caveat the article must keep honest:** row 8/9 come from a statute-text mirror

  article cites the section, not the mirror.
- **Applicability is the story, so keep the boundaries exact:** Oregon's cap is written for
  "contracts for construction and home improvement" (a house is inside it). Minnesota's cap binds a
  "building and construction contract" (a house is inside it). California's cap spells out that an
  ordinary house is outside it. Virginia's cap is public-procurement law, so it binds public bodies.
  Do not smooth these into "most states cap retainage at 5%".
