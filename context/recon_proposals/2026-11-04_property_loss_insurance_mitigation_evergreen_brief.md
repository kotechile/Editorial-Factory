**The arithmetic the article must defend (recompute, never restate from the draft).** For a house whose current rebuild cost is $600,000, the amount of insurance required to reach the 80 percent line is $480,000. At a limit of $420,000 — 70 percent of rebuild cost, and 87.5 percent of the required $480,000 — a $60,000 repair with a $2,500 deductible settles at $57,500 × 0.875 = $50,312.50, leaving $9,687.50 of the repair unpaid; the same loss settles in full at $57,500 once the limit reaches $480,000. On a total loss the limit is all there is: $420,000 against a $600,000 rebuild is a $180,000 gap, and no ratio applies. Closing the gap from $420,000 to $480,000 is a $60,000 (14.3 percent) increase in Coverage A. At the industry's 2023 average claim of $20,062, the same 87.5 percent fraction pays $17,554.25, a $2,507.75 haircut on the smallest claim in the series.
