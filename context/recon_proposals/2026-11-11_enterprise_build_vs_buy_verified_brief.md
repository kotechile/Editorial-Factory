## Binding rulings for the drafter

1. **These are two vendors' own tables, not a market price.** AWS publishes them about AWS's own stack and the Strands team publishes its own model card. Write "Amazon's engineers report", "the team publishes", never "the market price of ticket classification is".
2. **$500 is a utilisation result, not a price.** The page's own words tie it to burst headroom. Never print $500 as the cost of the workload without saying it reflects the headroom they keep.
3. **The 44x compares two list prices on one team's ticket volume.** It is not a portable saving, and the same page says the swap works only for a closed choice at high volume.
4. **No accuracy comparison exists between Sonnet and E4B on the page.** Row 8 is the authors' qualitative claim. Do not invent a percentage, and do not present the JevBench numbers as a Sonnet comparison — that benchmark scores the decision models alone.
5. **The instance-hour figure is the AWS list price for `ml.g5.xlarge` in us-east-1.** Do not generalise it to other hardware, regions, or a reserved or committed-use rate.
6. **Costs exclude the Lambda and exclude engineering time to build the endpoint, the CDK stack and the label rewrite.** Say so once; do not price the labour the page never prices.
7. **The $12,300-a-year floor is desk arithmetic** on the published hourly rate and a 8,760-hour year, and it assumes the box is rented continuously. Label it as arithmetic.
8. **The JSON key-order finding is the authors' own**, measured on their model: "up to 0.19" is a probability shift, not an error rate. Do not turn it into a failure percentage.
