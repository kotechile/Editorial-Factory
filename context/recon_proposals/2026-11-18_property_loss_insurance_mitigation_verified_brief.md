## Rulings to the drafter

1. **"Six" is desk counting, and it must read as a list, not as a published figure.** The TDI sentence gives "insufficient defensible space; maintenance, occupancy, or vacancy issues; roof condition; or tree overhang". Counting the items a homeowner can change gives six: defensible space, maintenance, occupancy, vacancy, roof condition, tree overhang. Never write that TDI says "six"; write that the definition names those things, and let the count be visibly ours.
2. **The reason table is not a count of decisions.** Row 6 is binding: a company may attach several reasons to one policy, and the page itself says the reasons report "isn't the total number of declinations, nonrenewals, and cancellations". Do not derive a claim about how many policies were dropped from the 289,218 rows. The rows are reason lines; that is all they are.
3. **Do not label the numeric codes.** The dataset's `action_type`, `policy_type` and `reason_code` values (80/81/82; 1–6; letter strings such as `Y000000000`) have no public code-to-label map that this run could retrieve. Use only the counts in rows 12 and 13, described as rows and policy types. Any sentence of the form "X% were non-renewals" would be invented.
4. **The tool's launch date is context, not a finding.** The page was launched 2026-09-23 and personal-auto data was added 2026-10-15, both from secondary write-ups; the page itself carries neither date. If either date is used, attribute it to coverage, or leave it out.
5. **Row 14 and 15 are the other desk's numbers.** If the article uses the national non-renewal figure at all, attribute it to the NAIC through the trade write-up and keep it in one framing sentence. It may not carry an argument.
6. **The clock is an observation, dated today.** "Newest notice month on file is 07/2026" is true as of 2026-11-18 and must be written as an observation with that date attached. Do not say TDI has failed to update the page — the page says only that it updates when data arrives.
7. **Nothing in this file supports a dollar figure.** No premium, no claim severity, no mitigation cost comes out of these sources. Any payback arithmetic must be clearly desk arithmetic on published numbers, or omitted.
