## Gate rulings

1. **Dual-anchor gate PASS.** Anchor A carries 8 VERIFIED claims (A1–A7, A9); Anchor B carries 4 (B1–B4). Zero REMOVED. One DERIVED/FLAGGED row (A8).
2. **Do not call Ramp's sample "the market".** Ramp's own methodology says the index covers "more than 85,000 American businesses" using its card and bill-pay data, and that the token metrics come from the subset using its Token Spend Management product, which "is not representative of Ramp's total AI spend volume, though results are directionally similar." Write "US businesses on Ramp", never "the US economy".
3. **The 1.65% and the 21% are different bases** (see A8). The article may put them in one sentence as two published readings. Any volume figure derived from them must be labelled an estimate with the division shown once.
4. **The 89% is "limited or redesigned", an OR.** Do not write "cut" in the sentence that carries the 89%; do not write "abandoned". And carry B4's counterweight: 55% still increased AI spending, so redesign is not retreat.
5. **The 40% line is an OR list** (processing frequency, model size, context size, output volume). Never attribute the whole 40% to context size alone, and never claim 40% of firms reduced all four.
6. **Cloudera is a vendor of a data and AI platform** and the study is commissioned by it; the 19% monitoring figure runs against its own sales narrative, which is worth saying in the piece. The 89% figure is self-reported and includes light-touch limits.
7. **A3's 80% is revenue OpenAI and Anthropic earn from Ramp customers**, not their global revenue.
8. **Derived ratio (optional):** $10,200 ÷ $16.32 = 625× — desk arithmetic on A2; if used, label it as arithmetic on two medians, not a published figure.
9. **Nothing may rest on a causal claim** that falling prices produced the flat bill, or that the redesign produced the price fall. The two sources are contemporaneous readings; the piece argues about what the pair implies, not what caused what.
10. **The word "savings" is ours, not the sources'.** Neither source calls the redesign a saving; if the piece uses the word it must be in an argument the piece is making, attributed to no one.
