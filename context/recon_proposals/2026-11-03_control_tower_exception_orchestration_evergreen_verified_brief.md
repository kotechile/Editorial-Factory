## Notes for the drafter

- The load-bearing standard is USP `<1079.2>` / `<659>`; both chapters are PDFs the verifier cannot read (`unreachable`), so every quotation is through a page that quotes the chapter, and the article must say so once rather than implying it read the pharmacopoeia directly. Do not call a vendor page "the standard".
- Row 4 (the 2–8°C allowance) is the most useful sentence in the brief and cannot carry a gate row; quote it verbatim under citation [1].
- Row 9's budget figures are the ELPRO page's own worked example. Never present them as a product's specification; the rule is that a budget comes from the product's own potency testing.
- Row 11 is the only automation figure in the corpus for this decision and it is a vendor's joint presentation. Use it as the ROI frame ("the product is a release decision"), never as a benchmark of the industry.
- 21 CFR 211.142(b) and 21 CFR 1.908 are the two regulatory sentences that make the monitoring obligation real; both fetch cleanly at `ecfr.gov` and belong in Sources, not in the numbers block.
- Do not derive a percentage from any pair of figures in this brief. There is no ratio in it that the sources state.
