## Gate rulings for the drafter

1. **Label each leg's provenance.** Rows 1–4 are a benchmark measurement. Rows 5–8 are a *counterfactual
   replay* over those same traces, not a deployment — say so. Rows 9–12 are a vendor lab's own study,
   published by the lab, 1,620 trials, no third-party replication.
2. **Do not merge the two studies' numbers.** 68.8% / 46.7% / 27.2% belong to the benchmark; 2.9% /
   3.3% / 2.7% belong to the replay; 81.0% / 18.1% / 37.2% / 43.1% belong to the revocation study.
3. **No derived arithmetic.** State 1,556 and 19,916 side by side; do not invent a multiple. Do not
   compute a "reduction" the papers do not state.
4. **Two `%` conventions.** The paper prints Table 4 as fractions (0.328); write them as 32.8% only if
   you also keep the paper's own 2% admissibility line beside it, or quote the fractions as printed.
5. **Nothing about prompt engineering as a cure.** The only prompt-rule evidence is row 9, which is a
   *revocation* study: a rule cut the bypass rate but did not stop it.
6. **The article may not claim any system was compromised.** All three sources measure synthetic or
   replayed traces; no named company suffered an incident in this evidence set.
