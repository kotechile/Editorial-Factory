**Claims explicitly refused for want of a retrievable primary:**
- Any figure for the *share of enterprises* that currently allocate AI spend per business unit. No fetchable survey states one; the Foundation page states the *capability gap*, not a rate.
- Any dollar cost-per-business-unit or per-query number of our own. The harness share is a working-group range, not a measurement, and the article must label it as an estimate.
- Any claim that Amazon, OpenAI or Anthropic offer a native business-unit field. Each offers keys, projects, workspaces or tags that the buyer must populate; a business unit is never a native dimension.
