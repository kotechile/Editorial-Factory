## Binding rulings (write these into the copy; the gates cannot read them)

- **FLAG 1 — do not attach the 30 cents to the current model.** The Register's sentence prices **GLM-5.1** ("self hosting GLM 5.1 cost about a tenth"), while the 56-GPU fleet is described as serving **GLM-5.3**. Write "Revolut's published figure is 30 cents per million tokens, quoted for its GLM-5.1 deployment" — never "GLM-5.3 costs 30 cents".
- **FLAG 2 — $5.00 is Mapbox's entry band, not a flat rate.** The table steps down to $4.00, $3.00 and $2.50 at higher volumes. Say "the entry rate" or quote the band; never write "Mapbox charges $5 per 1,000" as though it applied at every volume.
- **FLAG 3 — the 100x is the author's per-1,000 comparison, not a measured bill.** It rests on a 5 MB-per-view assumption. On Mapbox's own tiers, 100,000 loads in a month is 50,000 free plus 50,000 at $5.00 per 1,000 = $250, against about 500 GB of egress at $0.01/GB = about $5 — roughly 50x on that month. If the article states a multiple, it states the author's 100x **per 1,000 loads** and, separately, this month's arithmetic labelled as ours.
- **FLAG 4 — the 200 vs 800 tokens a second is the speaker's account.** The Register reports the higher figure as what engineers "signing in after 5 pm" get. It is an operator's description of contention, not a published peak/off-peak curve; never draw a curve from it.
- **FLAG 5 — the map is one individual's side project.** It is a hands-on operator record of a build, not an enterprise deployment, and neither the $5 nor the 100x generalises to corporate map spend. The desk ran neither stack: no "we tested" claim anywhere.
- **FLAG 6 — 36 engineers per card is our division.** 2,000 ÷ 56 ≈ 35.7. If used, write "about 36" and attribute the division to us.
- **Not cited, carried as context only:** the 2026-11-12/13 Microsoft 365 outage (about 20 hours, 140,000 reports from 74 countries) is recorded in this run's signals file only through a tertiary encyclopedia entry, which `source_gate` bars; it must not appear in `## Sources` and no figure from it may enter the body. The Gartner "token costs surpass salaries by 2028" release is dated 2026-06-24 and is out of window — it is not usable at all.
- **Prohibited framings:** "self-hosting is cheaper" as a general claim (false at low volume); "unlimited tokens" without the cap (Revolut explicitly caps context and targets a latency floor); any suggestion that Mapbox is overcharging (its unit buys unbounded tile work, which is the point of the piece).
