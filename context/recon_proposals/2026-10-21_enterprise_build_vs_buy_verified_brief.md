## Gate rulings for the drafter

1. **The strategy association is an association.** The report's own words: "Building from scratch, then, may be less a consequence of absent strategy than an unacknowledged substitute for it". Never write that building causes a strategy gap, and never write that a written strategy would have prevented a build.
2. **Do not use the publisher's headline as a finding.** The report's key takeaway reads "Teams are buying, not building" — that is a vendor of deployment tooling summarising its own survey, and it is the frame the desk is testing, not a measured fact. The numbers (51.2 / 31.2 / 17.5, 58.8 / 47.8 / 33.3) are the evidence; name the publisher and its commercial interest in the catch.
3. **"23" is a modal count, not an average.** Write "the most common number of features a platform team owns rose from 4 to 23", never "the average platform has 23 features".
4. **Gartner's 51% is an expectation, not a measurement.** Write "51% of CIOs expected AI to increase the total cost of ownership", never "AI raised TCO for 51% of enterprises". Likewise A1 is a self-report of realized value.
5. **Use the computed multiple, not the report's rounding.** "about 3.5 times the rate for tool buyers" (57.9 ÷ 16.7). Do not write "four times" as the desk's arithmetic; if the report's phrase is quoted, attribute it.
6. **Never cite**: the Gartner neocloud-impairment release of 2026-10-14 (published by another vertical on 2026-10-20), the Stack Overflow 2026 survey (anchored by this vertical on 2026-10-07), any Retool figure (out of window), or the Microsoft EWS timeline.
7. **Excluded prior-edition figures:** none retained. The report's 2025 comparators are used only as the feature-count baseline (4), which is B4's own sentence.
8. **Sample honesty:** 379 practitioners, four-month fielding, optional questions, research panel — one clause in the catch, not in the lead.
9. **No derived sums** beyond R1. Do not compute a cost figure, a maintenance multiplier, or a payback from these numbers; the desk's 3.8× field note may be named only as a desk field note, with no arithmetic attached.
