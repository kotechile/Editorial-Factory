## Rulings for the drafter (do not cross these)

1. **The 60% is a stress profile, not a field rate.** The injected faults were drawn from the ecosystem's own incident mix, which the paper calls "permanent-heavy". Write "under faults drawn from the ecosystem's own incident mix" — never "agents fail 60% of the time in production".
2. **MICA's headline number is divergence, not defeat.** 94.3% is the share of paired episodes whose trajectories diverge — it is not a success rate, not a failure rate, and it does not say one interface wins. The paper's own example has MCP *ahead* on WebShop and far behind on ALFWorld at the same parameter count. Never write "MCP is 94.3% worse".
3. **"Up to 9.0 points" and "up to 27.1k tokens" are best-case across the six settings and three environments.** Keep "up to"; never state them as averages.
4. **The scale null result is about two gpt-oss backbones, not models in general.** The paper says "the two gpt-oss backbones we test"; write "a 20B and a 120B from one model family", never "bigger models don't help".
5. **The stack result is "suggestive" in the authors' own word.** One significant and one marginal interaction (p = 0.048, p = 0.074). Report it with that hedge; do not upgrade it to proof.
6. **Do not merge the two studies' model tiers.** MICA's suite tops out at Qwen3-32B, DeepSeek-v4-Flash and Gemini-2.5-Flash; MCP-Injector tested 20B and 120B gpt-oss. Neither tested a frontier reasoning model, and the Scale Labs result says interface sensitivity shrinks as capability rises. Carry that as the counter-argument, not as a footnote to hide.
7. **No invented mechanism for why the interface effect reverses.** The paper's own attribution is "presentation, realization, and feedback"; do not add a causal story about schema verbosity or token counts beyond what R2/R3 state.
8. **DART preserves a validated semantic action** and repairs only its interface-specific expression — it is not "the model re-plans", and it is not a product.
9. **Every bracket number in the body maps to this table.** No figure from the abstracts may be recomputed into a new ratio (no "1.7× the score", no "2.5× more resilient").
10. **MCP-Injector is a NeurIPS 2026 poster (peer-reviewed) and MICA is an arXiv preprint (not peer-reviewed).** Say which is which once, plainly, in the catch.
