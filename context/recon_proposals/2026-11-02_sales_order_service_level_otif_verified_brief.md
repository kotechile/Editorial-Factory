## Gate rulings carried to the drafter
1. **Row 10 is the article.** Kroger's own compliance letter says "many of these fees will not apply if Kroger manages your inbound lanes." Quote it as written; do not paraphrase it into "fees stop" (the source says many, not all) and do not extend it to lanes Kroger does not manage.
2. **The policy's vintage must be stated once.** The fee schedule is a standing document signed 2024-12-02 with terms effective 2025-01-01, and it says "This policy will be reviewed annually and is subject to change." SPS restated the current terms on 2026-10-13. Present the thresholds as Kroger's published program, attribute the restatement to SPS, and never date the terms as new this month.
3. **"10%" is the publisher's own arithmetic, not mine.** Grocery Dive's section heading reads "10% — Percentage of products Kroger aims to directly source by 2029" while the body says "less than 1%" growing "tenfold". Say "tenfold, from under 1%" or "about a tenth, on the company's own framing" — never "Kroger will source exactly 10%".
4. **Grapes: use Grocery Dive's wording.** It says "approximately 40% of its imported grape supply ... with support from service partners". The Shelby Report's version says "40 percent of its imported grape volume ... for the November 2026 season" — that season detail is not in the primary coverage and is **barred**: do not print "for the November 2026 season".
5. **Bars.** No "Kroger will replace suppliers" — the source says a tenth of items, and the other nine-tenths stay on the vendor lane. No claim that the ORAD program is being abolished. No claim that Kroger-managed lanes carry no fees at all (the letter says "many of these fees"). No claim that 40% of groceries go direct (it is 40% of *imported grapes*).
6. **Dollar figures barred.** The "$4 billion savings" and "$2 billion in cost of goods" figures appear only in secondary coverage (The Shelby Report, a LinkedIn post) and not in the release or in Grocery Dive's own reporting; they are **not to be used**. Likewise **bar** any multiplication of 3% × revenue, any annualised deduction total, and the Cleo Research "$100 billion a year" OTIF figure (anchored by a sibling article on 2026-10-27).
7. **Rows 16 and 17 are SPS estimates, not measurements.** If either appears, it carries SPS's name and its hedge in the same sentence ("SPS's own estimate", "a planning range, not an industry study"). Prefer to leave them out; the article does not need them.
8. **Do not compare the deduction rate to a fill rate.** A deduction percentage of invoice value and a fill percentage of units are different quantities; the piece may set the 3% fee beside the 95% threshold, never convert one into the other.
9. **Wholesale/retail inventory figures are all-merchant-wholesaler aggregates**, not food distribution, and the Census release excludes manufacturers' sales branches and offices. If used, say "merchant wholesalers across the economy", never "grocery wholesalers".
10. **No hands-on claim.** This desk has no Kroger data and no supplier files; write from the documents.
