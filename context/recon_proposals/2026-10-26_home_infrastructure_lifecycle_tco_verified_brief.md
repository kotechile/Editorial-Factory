## Notes for the drafter
- **Never present the LIRA figures as something that happened.** Every one is a projection for 2026-Q4 through 2027-Q3, published 2026-10-22. Write "projects", "expects", "the October release puts".
- **The 7.5 percent is ours, not theirs.** Name the two inputs in the same paragraph ("spending growing 1.5 percent, materials up 9.7 percent") before the division, and say the division is the article's own arithmetic.
- **Producer prices are not installed cost.** BLS prices producers' receipts; a contractor's quote adds labour, permitting, truck rolls and margin. Use the PPI to establish direction and magnitude of the materials line, never as "the price of a roof".
- **Copper wire and cable is a distinct series from copper and brass mill shapes.** Do not merge them; the number this brief verified is WPU1026, +23.0 percent.
- **AHRI is corroboration and it is directional.** Do not write that installations fell; write that shipments of gas furnaces and water heaters fell.
- **No hands-on claim.** This desk has no test bench — no "we tested", no "our installer found".
- **Excluded, deliberately:** the 2026-10-19 retrofit-gap paper (already anchored that day's article), the ACHR October price-increase list (likewise), and any prior-edition LIRA figure — the blog is the October 2026 release and its own $524B/$527B chart is the figure set for this edition.
