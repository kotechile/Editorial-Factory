## Gate rulings for the drafter

1. **State the two ASR figures side by side, never as a single derived percentage.** "19.6% against 60.5%" is the claim; do not write "a 41-point gap" or "two-thirds less" as though the paper stated it. If a difference is stated, label it as the desk's own arithmetic on the paper's table.
2. **Row C4 is a scope limit, not a footnote.** The repair numbers come from one open model family (Qwen3.5-9B, plus a 35B replication) on three synthetic drift types. The draft must say that plainly — this is evidence that the layer exists and is cheap, not a frontier-model benchmark.
3. **Row B4 is a single named case inside a corpus of 18.** Do not generalize the NeMo Guardrails mechanism to "every framework ships broken monitors"; the paper's claim is 18 confirmed cases across 11 project groups, each with a fixed revision, negative controls and a frozen audit.
4. **GatewayBench's 7 gateways are not named in this brief's claims.** Do not imply that a specific product is safe or unsafe: keep the claim at the architecture level (flattened list vs on-demand discovery), which is what the paper's mean rows support.
5. **The "no owner / no release process" line is the desk's reading, not a finding.** Label it as such in the body.
6. **The task-time and token numbers are one configuration.** 233.3k tokens per turn is the flattened mean at 1,500 injected tools under GPT-5.4; do not present it as the typical bill.
7. **Do not cite a fourth source.** Every URL in the body must already exist in `2026-10-19_agentic_ai_signals.md`; those three arXiv URLs are the whole admissible set for this piece.
