## Audit notes for the drafter (carried from the gate fetch, not the gate marker)

1. **Two of the three duct figures are one page's report of field research, not an agency measurement.** Row 1's "15-20% on average" is the inspection standard's summary of California research; row 2's 12% / more than 40% is a single 1995 study (Modera and Jump) measured in a Sacramento attic. Write them as field findings with their names and years attached — never as a national average, and never as "studies show" without the citation sitting in the same paragraph.
2. **The 350 cfm/ton figure is a commissioning criterion, not a rating.** The same page applies it to indoor coil airflow at system acceptance ("indoor coil airflow must be greater than 350 cfm/ton"); do not present it as a manufacturer specification for any particular unit.
3. **SEER2 14.3 / HSPF2 7.5 is a manufacturing floor, not a promise of savings.** It dates from the January 1, 2023 compliance date, applies to split-system heat pumps, and is stated per product class in the regulation. Do not convert SEER to SEER2 (the test procedures differ), do not annualise it into a dollar payback, and do not imply a specific percentage saving from replacing any specific unit.
4. **Any arithmetic is labelled as ours.** The only permitted derived figure is the share relationship: 15-20% of the space-conditioning slice, which is 52% of household energy (EIA, 2020) — i.e. roughly 8% to 10% of the whole household energy bill, written as the desk's own arithmetic on two cited figures, with both citations in the same sentence.
5. **No product prices and no incentive dollars.** No duct-sealing quote, no equipment price, no tax credit may appear — the federal 25C credits expired December 31, 2025 (recorded in the 2026-10-12 home_equity_tco verified brief). The article's actionability is the scope and the test, not a price.
