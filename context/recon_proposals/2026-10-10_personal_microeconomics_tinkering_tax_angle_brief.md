**Angle Type:** Single-Signal

**Scores:** N=8.5 A=9.5 S=8.0 → Composite=8.65

**Hook:** On October 2, 2026, Nabu Casa announced it is renaming Home Assistant Cloud to Home Assistant Link. "We are renaming Home Assistant Cloud to Home Assistant Link, because we hate clouds," founder Paulus Schoutsen told The Verge. "Big tech ruined the cloud, so we're out" (https://www.theverge.com/tech/1003936/home-assistant-says-big-tech-ruined-the-cloud-so-were-out).

**Tension:** The open-source self-hosting camp has spent a decade arguing the cloud tax is a bad deal — and this week its most visible platform put that on a nameplate, renaming its own paid service to strip the word "cloud" out of it. The timing matters because the rent model it is rejecting has been migrating from content (subscriptions) to hardware people already paid for: cameras that stop recording without a plan, thermostats clouded off in a region, ovens bricked when a vendor sunsets a server. Schoutsen's framing — a device that becomes "a dumb oven that cost $1,500" — is the same argument the founder voice makes about the tinkering tax, aimed the other way at the vendor: the price on the box is not the price of ownership. The friction point: the "own it locally" path is not a price cut, it is a swap of a cash bill for a maintenance bill, and that maintenance bill is also moving — Let's Encrypt is shortening the certificate lifetimes that every self-hosted reverse proxy depends on from 90 days to 64 (then 45 by 2028), which raises the renewal cadence for anyone who hardcoded their cron jobs. Both camps are repricing at once.

**Target reader:** systems_tinkerer_pro

**Single claim to defend:** A cloud dependency is a recurring rent on hardware you have already bought — and the honest counterweight is not "self-hosting is free" but "self-hosting is a maintenance bill," which is why the two numbers a self-hoster should actually track are the monthly fee they are renting and the hours their own renewal and update automation saves them.
