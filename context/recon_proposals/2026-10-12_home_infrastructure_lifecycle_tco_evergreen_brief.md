## Audit notes for the drafter (carried from the gate fetch, not the gate marker)

1. **The two 13-percent sentences on one page disagree about the base.** The first reads "lowers household energy bills by an average of up to 13 percent nationwide when replacing single-pane windows" (base = the household bill, best case = single-pane); the second reads "shrink energy bills by an average of up to 13% percent on heating and cooling costs nationwide, compared to non-certified products" (base = heating-and-cooling costs). The draft must carry the **single-pane qualifier** and must not present either number as the average household's bill saving. The sentence "13% of the bill" is only true as the *best case*.
2. **The savings numbers are programme estimates, not measured field trials.** Do not describe them as measured outcomes, and do not annualise them into a dollar payback — no window price is in this table, so no payback figure may be invented.
3. **Any arithmetic is labelled as ours.** The only derived figure permitted is the share relationship: 13% of heating-and-cooling costs against a 52% heating-and-cooling share of total household energy (≈7% of the whole bill at best), written as the desk's own arithmetic, the way the 2026-10-12_silent-15400-home-loss evergreen wrote its expected-loss line.
4. **No incentive dollars.** The federal 25C credits expired December 31, 2025 (recorded in the 2026-10-12 home_equity_tco verified brief), and the DOE savings-hub pages still list credits — so no window rebate or tax-credit figure may appear in this draft.
5. **Every figure in the body must appear in this table.** No new numbers, names or dates.
