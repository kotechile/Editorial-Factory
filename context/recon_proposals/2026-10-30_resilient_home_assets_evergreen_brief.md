## Notes for the drafting stage (not gate fields)
- **Not a review.** No product is named, no price is quoted, no hands-on claim is made. Keep it that way: the moment the piece ranks brands it belongs in the review pipeline.
- **The honest reading of the "ten times" figure is conditional, not absolute.** IBHS's sentence is "roofs exposed to both natural weathering and two simulated sub-severe hail events became up to ten times more susceptible **compared to new shingles**". Do not write it as "your roof is ten times weaker" — write it as the comparison it is, and keep the word "up to".
- **Do not convert 2.8 percent and $20,062 into a single expected-loss dollar figure as if it were a published statistic.** If the piece multiplies them, it must say so in the sentence ("about one home in 36 a year against a $20,062 average claim") and label it the desk's arithmetic from two ISO figures on one page.
- **Texas's credit is a TWIA and carrier rule, not a national one.** The article must say who the rule binds (TWIA policyholders in Texas; each carrier's own credit elsewhere) rather than implying every state mandates a Class 4 discount.
- **The label elements are the actionable rule.** UL label + class + manufacturer name + year manufactured + brand name on the product, plus TDI form PC068 filed by the contractor — a roof that passes UL 2218 but is not labelled that way earns nothing. That is the article's one checklist.
