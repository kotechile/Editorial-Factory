**Decision the reader is facing:** Where the enterprise enforces authorization over a RAG corpus — by partitioning the index at ingest per tenant or classification boundary, or by filtering what the retriever returned — and therefore how many indexes to fund, what ACL metadata every chunk must carry, and what happens to a document's blast radius on the day one document is misclassified or poisoned. **Thesis:** A document's blast radius is the index it lives in, not the filter the query applies afterwards — a chunk's embedding turns back into 92% of its text, five injected documents steer a database of millions 90% of the time, and per-tenant partitioning costs a fraction of a node — so the number of indexes an enterprise runs is a governance control, and an ACL that exists only in the query path is decoration rather than a boundary.
