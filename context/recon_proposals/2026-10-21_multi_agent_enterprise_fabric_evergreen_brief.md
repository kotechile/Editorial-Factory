**Decision the reader is facing:** When the seconds-to-minutes an agent spends reasoning sit between the read that justified an action and the write that commits it, and another writer — a peer agent, a human, a scheduled sync — changes the same record inside that gap, does the fabric let the agent's write land, abort and replan on any version change, or revalidate only the premises the decision actually used? It bites the first time two agents hold authority over the same purchase order, vendor record or employee object.
