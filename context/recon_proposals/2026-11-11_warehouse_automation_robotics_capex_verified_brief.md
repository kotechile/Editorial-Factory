## Binding rulings for the drafter

1. **71.3% is gross margin after cost of materials only — not "profit on the hardware."** AutoStore's line is "cost of materials", and the quarterly report states the figures are unaudited. Write it as "the share of each revenue dollar left after the cost of materials" and never as a markup, a multiple, or "what the robots really cost to build". The article's argument does not need the stronger version.
2. **Order intake is not revenue.** USD 177.6M was booked in the quarter; USD 192.1M was recognised. Do not add them, do not treat the difference as growth, and do not call the intake "sales".
3. **The 94% and the one-in-four are marginals of the same 250 respondents and no cross-tab is published.** The release never says how many of the regretful buyers also say they cannot switch. Never write "of those who regret it, 94% cannot leave". Both figures are respondents' *stated opinion* of switching cost, not a measured cost.
4. **Exotec sells the alternative.** It is a warehouse robotics vendor and the report is its own marketing asset; the survey's three causes (lifetime cost, accountability, readiness) map onto Exotec's own pitch. Attribute every figure to "Exotec's survey" and carry the vendor relationship in the same breath, once.
5. **"10 to 15 years" is Exotec's framing, not a survey answer.** The release says the switching cost is "leaving most buyers with the system they chose for the next 10 to 15 years". Attribute it; do not present it as a measured equipment life.
6. **Proclamation 11077 imposed no tariff.** It sets no rate, sets no date for a rate, and the only clock is the 180-day report. Say "no duty", "negotiations", "the report is due in April 2027", and "tariffs remain available" — never "tariffs were imposed" or "a robotics tariff".
7. **The Logiqs per-site figure is desk arithmetic on two stated numbers** ("up to EUR 30 million", "approximately 30 customer sites") and the price buys a company with 60 employees, software and horticultural systems, not just sites. Write it as an order-of-magnitude reading, never as "AutoStore paid €1M for a site".
8. **No hands-on claim.** The desk has not visited a warehouse, operated a fleet or seen a contract. Nothing in this piece may say "we tested", "in our experience" or "when we deployed".
9. **The three percent-class figures must not be blended.** 71.3% (gross margin), 94% (stated switching cost), 65% (TCO overrun) and 60/27/22 are separate questions from separate legs; one percentage per sentence, and only one percentage in the numbers block.
10. **Derived figures used in the body:** 25.2 points of order-intake growth in dollars (177.6 − 152.4); USD 50M of guidance raised (750 − 700); 63 of 250 respondents is one in four; 2027-04-26 is 180 days after signature. All computed in-session, all stated as computations where they appear.
