**Decision the reader is facing:** Whether to keep buying hosted CI minutes or move the pipeline onto self-hosted runners on rented servers — and, if building, what monthly CI volume one hour a month of runner upkeep has to clear before the saving is real. It bites when someone reads the CI line on the cloud bill as "compute we could run ourselves" and the utilisation of the rented box is never in the spreadsheet.

**Thesis:** A hosted CI minute and a rented runner are the same compute sold two ways — $0.006 per Linux two-core minute against $0.02679 per server-hour — so the build decision is a utilisation test, not a price comparison: a self-hosted lane must be busy about 7% of the month just to break even on the hardware, and every hour a month of engineer upkeep moves that bar roughly 182 core-hours, which is why most pipelines should keep buying the meter.
