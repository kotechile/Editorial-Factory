## Flags and constraints for the drafter
- **FLAGGED (direction of the 10× claim).** IBHS's sentence compares weathered-and-hit panels *to new shingles*. It does not say a roof loses 90% of its strength, and it is a laboratory programme result, not a field failure rate. The article must carry "up to" and the comparison, and must not convert it into "your roof is ten times weaker".
- **FLAGGED (scope of the Texas rule).** TDI's page describes the credit regime for Texas, and the labelling/form requirements specifically for TWIA policyholders. It is not a national mandate; elsewhere the credit is carrier-set or absent. Do not write "the state pays" or "every insurer must".
- **REMOVED — the per-state discount percentages.** No regulator or statute carries them; the only pages stating them are roofing lead-generation sites. The article says the amount is carrier-set and names no percentage.
- **REMOVED — the Class 4 cost premium.** No fetchable primary prices it; the upgrade price is a contractor quote, not a published figure. The article therefore gives no payback arithmetic and names no product.
- **Money rule:** `$20,062` may appear in at most two sections. Keep it in **By the numbers** and refer to "the average claim" by name elsewhere.
- **Not a review:** no product is named, no price quoted, no hands-on claim made (AGENTS.md §14 — a product ranking belongs to `skills/review_comparison.md`).
