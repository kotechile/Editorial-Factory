# Angle Brief: enterprise_build_vs_buy — 2026-09-23

**Winner:** AI made building cheap — and made the maintenance bill worse. Five price changes from three AI vendors in eight weeks, "every one a maintenance ticket for an internal tool."

**Scores:** N=9 A=8 S=9 → Composite=8.7

**Hook:** In roughly eight weeks this summer, three AI vendors moved their prices five times — OpenAI cut one model 80%, another 20%, then trimmed its flagship 20%+ three weeks later. Each move lands as a maintenance ticket in some engineering team's backlog.

**Tension:** AI collapsed the *upfront* cost of building internal tools (days, not quarters), so "I could build that in a week" became a real sentence. But it also turned the thing being built into a moving target: models, prices, and providers now change monthly. The build-vs-buy decision — which used to turn on sticker price — now turns entirely on the maintenance tail, and that tail got worse, not better.

**Target reader:** eng_leader (senior practitioner who ships; wants concrete numbers, decision frameworks, Monday-morning moves).

**Single claim to defend:** Because AI vendors now reprice monthly, every internally-built AI tool carries a permanent maintenance tail of $125k–$250k/year — so the 2026 build-vs-buy decision is won or lost on the tail, not the upfront build.
