## Gate rulings for the drafter
- **The 11% → nearly 100% contrast may not become a fourth figure elsewhere.** It is the lead's hook and one numbers bullet only. Do not repeat it in the mechanism section.
- **Do not merge the pooling sentence's three quantities:** 46% is tool CPU *demand*, 13% is *worst-case tool latency*, 99% is *serving throughput retained*. One sentence, three nouns, no "46% faster".
- **Do not merge the harvesting sentence either:** 95% is standalone throughput recovered, 30% is host utilization gained, 3% is the slowdown ceiling for the agent.
- **The 71.4% and the 3.5x are one result, stated once each.** "Up to" must survive any rewrite — the paper's best case, under contention, against eager release.
- **120,000 / 30,000–300,000 work units are the scheduler's own settings, not a recommendation.** They may illustrate the shape of a bounded budget; they may not be presented as a setting a reader should adopt.
- **The 60-retries-per-minute figure is the book's illustrative example**, not a measured optimum.
- **Attribution:** arXiv:2608.04458v1 — Jirong Yang, Peizhe Liu, Chaojie Zhang (Microsoft Azure), Jovan Stojkovic (UT Austin), 5 Aug 2026; arXiv:2609.10964v1 — Bochao Feng, Jianjiang Li (USTB), Haojie Wang, Lin Qiao, Yinghui Li, Yukun Yan (Qiyuan Lab), Jidong Zhai (Tsinghua), 10 Sep 2026.
- **No hands-on claim.** The desk ran no hosts. Any arithmetic the article shows (a quota against a burst, in-flight work) must be visibly arithmetic.
- **The honest limit to carry into the catch:** the burst trace is one run inside a study whose fleet data is aggregate; in-process or remote-API tools change where the tail lives; role pooling assumes tool work shares the host with the scheduler.
