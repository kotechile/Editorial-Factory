## Gate rulings for the drafter

1. **The $1.2 million is the desk's arithmetic, not a source figure.** $700,000 (PA restitution, row 1) + $518,000 (MN restitution, row 7) = $1,218,000, described as "about $1.2 million" and attributed to this article's addition — never quoted as a state's number. The two offices' *totals* are different and must not be blended: Pennsylvania's order also carries $50,000 costs and $250,000 in suspended penalties; Minnesota's judgment also carries $518,000 in penalties and $33,000 in costs, and the AG's own headline rounds the whole to "$1.1 million". Never write "$1.2 million judgment" or "$1.2 million in penalties".
2. **Pennsylvania's conduct is alleged, not found.** The release says "alleged" for the sewer defects and the leak/heat-exchanger diagnoses, and the order states it is not an admission of a violation (row 6). The article must attribute every Pennsylvania fact to the Attorney General's allegations or to the consent order's terms; only the August 2023 summary-judgment findings are "entered findings" and they are about consumer-protection violations generally, not about the sewer diagnoses. Minnesota's judgment and guilty plea may be stated as fact (rows 7–12).
3. **Do not call the Pennsylvania defendant a fraudster or a fabricator.** The accurate, load-bearing formulation is that the state alleged the defects did not exist and that the diagnoses were discredited by third parties — the utility is the test, not the verdict.
4. **No hands-on claim and no price beyond the sources.** No repair price, no cost of a heat-pump replacement, no "we tested". The only money figures available are rows 1, 7 and the derived sum.
5. **The 13-month and 3-year intervals are derived arithmetic.** October 2025 → 2026-11-05 is about 13 months; August 2023 → 2026-10-21 is a little over three years. Both may be stated as intervals computed from the sources, never as a source's sentence.
6. **No claim about the recovery fund paying this case's victims.** Pietron was never licensed, so the fund's own terms do not reach his victims; the brief's point is that the licence is the fund's precondition, not that the fund failed.
7. **Characterisation of the second document.** For Minnesota "forged a phony invoice" is the Attorney General's own wording in a judgment — usable. For Pennsylvania the document class is a diagnosis/estimate for excavation; do not call it forged.
