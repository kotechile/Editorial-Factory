## Gate rulings carried to the drafter

1. **The 11-agent field run is one team's incident, dated 15 April 2026.** It may never be presented as an industry rate; the 72–100% figure is the study's own seven-scenario measurement and carries its own sample description ("5 to 50 concurrent agents").
2. **135,000 tokens is approximate** ("∼135 K"). Write it as "about 135,000", never as an exact cost or a dollar figure — the paper states no price.
3. **The failures in that run were thrown errors** (ECONNRESET, HTTP 502), so the article may not call them silent. The *capacity* signal is what is invisible; the deaths were loud.
4. **Admission control alone is insufficient** (81.8%) and retry is the ablation's most impactful primitive. Any rule that reads "cap concurrency" as the fix contradicts the source.
5. **The 72–100% / 0–18% pair is a between-condition comparison**, not an effect size on any deployment; keep both ends and the "under contention" qualifier.
6. **Little's Law rows are arithmetic, not measurement.** The AWS examples are worked illustrations published by an operator; the resulting thread counts are identities, and the article must not attribute them to a benchmark.
7. **"50 percent" is a client-perceived availability statement** conditional on median latency equalling the timeout — it may not be generalised to "a slow service is 50% down".
8. **The 3X → 1.1x pair is Google's retry accounting**, and both ends must be kept: the per-request cap alone yields just below 3X, the per-client 10% ratio yields ~1.1x. Do not print 3X without the ratio.
9. **1,024 connections per cluster is Envoy's default**, counted per cluster/upstream rather than per caller — it is a pool guard, not a fairness rule.
10. **No derived arithmetic is authorised** beyond the source's own sums: no cost-per-token, no failure-rate change (e.g. "cut failures by X points"), no payback.
11. **The paper is a preprint** (arXiv, submitted 18 April 2026, self-reported, validated against two local model servers). The catch must say so; the direction is supported by decades of queueing practice, the exact rates are unreplicated.
