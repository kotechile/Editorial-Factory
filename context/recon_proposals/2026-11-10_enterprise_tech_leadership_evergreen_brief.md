**Arithmetic performed in the brief (recompute in prose from the cited endpoints, never from the draft):**

- Rotation floor: 2 always-staffed seats ÷ 25% of an engineer's time = **8 engineers** single-site; dual-site is 6 per site per the same chapter.
- Page budget: 2 incidents per 12-hour shift × 2 shifts = **4 incidents/day = 28/week = 1,456/year**.
- Incident labour at the ceiling: 28 × 6 hours = **168 engineer-hours/week = 4.2 FTE**, which is **52.5%** of an eight-person team's 320-hour week — i.e. the page ceiling *is* the 50%-operational cap, reached from the other direction.
- Money: 6 hours × $65.38 = **$392.28 per incident**; 168 hours × $65.38 × 52 = **$571,159.68 a year** of incident work at the ceiling, against **$1,087,840** of payroll for the eight engineers who carry it (52.5% again).
- Service admission rule: 28 incidents/week ÷ measured pages-per-service-per-week = the number of paging deployables the rotation can absorb before it is over budget; at one page per service per week that is 28 services, at one a fortnight 56.
