# Angle Brief: supply_chain — 2026-09-07

**Winner:** The IEEPA tariff clawback got quiet — then it hit earnings. ~$18.7B has been disclosed across 101 public companies, and the single biggest recovery (Walmart's ~$2.9B) was only spoken on an earnings call, never filed.

**Scores:** N=7 · A=9 · S=9 → Composite=8.2 (≥8 → proceed)

**Hook:** As of Aug 28, public U.S. companies have disclosed about $18.7 billion in gross refunds of tariffs struck down by the Supreme Court's Feb. 20 IEEPA decision. Deere booked $382M year-to-date; Caterpillar's Q2 operating profit carried $392M. Walmart's CFO said on its Aug. 20 call that the retailer was eligible for roughly $2.9B — a figure that, at the time Calcbench tallied the data, appeared in no SEC filing at all, only the audio of the call.

**Tension:** The ruling killed the tariffs, but the refund is a slow, uneven rollout: ~$166B was collected, ~$104B had been accepted for processing by June 29, ~$71B disbursed. The biggest money is landing on the biggest importers (retailers + heavy-equipment OEMs), often as a one-time, non-recurring add-back CFOs can quietly absorb. Meanwhile the riskiest phase — finally-liquidated entries, Phase III — is unscheduled, and DOJ is appealing the universal-refund order, so smaller importers who didn't file protective actions may never see a dime. Who it helps: big importer-retailers and equipment OEMs. Who it hurts: everyone whose tariff-line was never a top-shelf refund (small/mid shippers, finally-liquidated entries).

**Target reader:** ops_leader (operations / supply-chain leader; business-operational, numerate, ROI-first; wants cost/risk framing, vendor-neutral, actionable)

**Single claim to defend:** IEEPA tariff refunds are real, material money now landing in major importers' P&Ls — at least ~$18.7B disclosed across 101 public companies, with ~$104B of ~$166B collected already accepted for processing — and the distribution is deeply uneven, which changes how an ops leader negotiates cost pass-through with vendors and how they should grade their own remaining exposure.
