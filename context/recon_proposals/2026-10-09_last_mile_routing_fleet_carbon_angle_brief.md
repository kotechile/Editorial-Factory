**Emergent Collision Point:** Diesel spent two weeks falling (down ~5% from the $6.53 record) yet is still almost 2.5x last year's price. The intuition is that a fuel bill that high should finally make electric trucks pencil out on energy cost alone. The Etsy pilot is the counter-evidence: the shippers funding electric freight are not relying on the pump-price delta at all — they are buying certificates to pay the gap. Signal A sets the fuel economics; Signal B shows the fuel economics still do not decide the switch.
**Hook:** Diesel dropped 33 cents in two weeks to $6.199 a gallon, and the first fleets to run Class 8 electric trucks on a real lane still need a certificate — not the fuel saving — to make the numbers work.
**Tension:** Carriers and CFOs read the diesel price as the electric-truck business case; the certificate market proves the business case lives on a carbon-accounting ledger, not a fuel ledger. Whoever owns the emissions accounting (shipper, carrier, or a GMA-style intermediary) decides who pays for the last mile's decarbonization — and the fuel bill is a distraction from that fight.
