## Gate rulings for the drafter

1. **No hands-on claim.** This run has no test bench. Nothing in the draft may say "we tested", "in our testing", "we installed" or imply first-hand device experience. The only first-person permitted is the observer voice.
2. **Do not claim the Marketplace is unsafe, and do not claim it is safe.** The verified position is narrower: the platform states it does not audit or support catalog content, and its security policy excludes malicious-integration attacks from scope. There is **no** verified instance of a malicious Marketplace/HACS project causing harm in this window; never imply one.
3. **The 2021 HACS disclosure is dated background.** If used, it must carry its year and the fact that it was a directory traversal reported in HACS and several custom integrations (https://www.home-assistant.io/blog/2021/01/22/security-disclosure). It is not evidence about the 2026 catalog.
4. **The foundation's kill-switch proposal is an open issue, not a shipped feature.** If mentioned: the Open Home Foundation roadmap issue asking to "centrally disable malicious or vulnerable integrations and Apps" is open, filed 2026-06-11 (https://github.com/OpenHomeFoundation/roadmap/issues/186), and today's mechanism is a Repairs notice via the `homeassistant_alerts` integration. Never describe it as a capability.
5. **Percentages and counts exactly as published** (rows 9–14). Print "11 of 80", "27 of 80", "a third", "21%", "25 of 80 against 52". Do not compute a new percentage from them.
6. **Makers, not devices.** CR surveyed manufacturers' disclosure practices, not device security outcomes. Never convert it into "one in six smart-home devices is insecure".
7. **Authorised arithmetic:** the 11 + 16 = 27 reconciliation only (row 11). No other derived sum, ratio, difference or complement is authorised, and no per-household saving, cost or downtime figure.
8. **Names and dates verbatim:** "Home Assistant", "HACS (Home Assistant Community Store)", "Marketplace", "Consumer Reports", 2026.11, 2026-10-01, 2026-10-15, 2026-10-16, 2026-10-17.
