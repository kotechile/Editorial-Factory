## Rulings for the drafter (binding)

1. **"No expected impact" is the port's statement about terminal productivity, not about a truck's day.** A5 and A11 do not contradict each other: one measures berth, yard and gate throughput, the other measures road time. The draft must not stage them as a contradiction and must not call the port wrong; the claim is that the cost lands outside what the port measured.
2. **Do not multiply the 8% drayage share by the 9% heavy-truck share.** They count different populations (drayage moves versus all vehicles on the bridge). Report each as the source states it, in its own sentence, and never derive a "trucks per day" figure.
3. **$753 million is the port's project figure; ABC7 rounds it to $750 million.** Use the port's $753 million once, and do not print both.
4. **The 45–60 minutes is an estimate by truckers, published by a forwarder, and the congestion surcharge has no published amount.** Carry it as an estimate with its source named, and say plainly that no published figure prices the surcharge. It may not carry arithmetic.
5. **The January surge is the article's inference, not a sourced fact.** The sources establish only that the exclusions and the fee suspension now lapse on 9/10 January 2027 and that the ports posted records in September. Write the inference as a conditional ("if the deadline pulls cargo forward…"), never as a forecast attributed to USTR, Caltrans or either port.
6. **Do not call the six-week programme a failure or a port mistake.** It was announced as mitigation for the closure's start. The finding is that the two calendars do not overlap; the planning consequence is the reader's.
7. **The 9 January date is the operative one for exclusions** (11:59 p.m. ET), and 10 January is the maritime-fee date. Use 9 January for the exclusions cliff and mention the fee suspension's 10 January date only if the sentence needs it.
8. **Nothing in this piece may rest on the ILWU Local 26 watchmen dispute.** It is a separate bargaining unit, already published on this front on 2026-11-03; do not mention it even as colour.
