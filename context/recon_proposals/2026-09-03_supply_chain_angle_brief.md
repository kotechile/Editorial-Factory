**Hook:** Beginning September 18, 2026, CBP will void an importer-of-record number — "invalid for any purpose, including entering imported merchandise into the United States" — when the Form 5106 information on file is inaccurate or incomplete. No grace period, no advance warning: the cargo stops at the port.

**Tension:** The rule (published Aug 19) is the sharpest edge of a 60-day enforcement wave that has stopped being "announced" and started being "operational." The shift: duties are now enforced at the paperwork and port-clearance layer, not just the bill of materials. Who it hurts: mid-market importers and 3PLs that run compliance through a broker and haven't audited their own IOR data. Who it helps: brokers, compliance software, and the domestic producers the duties protect.
