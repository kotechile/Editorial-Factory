## Authorised arithmetic (the only derived figures the draft/article may state)

- 75 min ÷ 12 (the 1:12 charging ratio) = **6.25 minutes of charging** — write "about six minutes".
- 6.25 min ÷ 60 × 7.2 kW = **0.75 kWh**; × 13.41¢ = **about 10 cents per robot-day**; × 260 working days = **about 26 dollars per robot-year**.
- 75 ÷ 480 = **about 16 extra robots per 100** if the gap is covered with machines instead of a charger.
- 3,000 cycles × 405 min = 1,215,000 min = **20,250 hours**; ÷ 2,080 h = **about 9.7 years** (one shift); ÷ 4,160 h = **about 4.9 years** (two shifts); ÷ 6,240 h = **about 3.3 years** (three shifts).
