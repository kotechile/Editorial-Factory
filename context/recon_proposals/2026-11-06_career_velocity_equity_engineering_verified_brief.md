## Drafting constraints that come out of the fact-check

1. **Never write a vesting duration as fact.** The company publishes none; one outlet reports "continued
   stock vesting" and an advisory firm reports 12 months. Write that the equity term is in the individual
   offer letter, and say plainly that the two public readings differ.
2. **Never present Carta's data as a Microsoft fact.** Carta's population is private companies on its
   platform; Microsoft is public and its grants are RSUs priced by a market. The article's claim is about
   the *direction of the pay market*, not about Microsoft's own grants.
3. **Say that seniority still pays more.** Row 4 exists so the piece cannot be read as "juniors now out-earn
   directors". The finding is a rate of change, and the article must say so in the same breath.
4. **The 39-week cap is a cash cap.** It is the standard severance formula reported by Business Insider from
   offers it reviewed, not a published policy; attribute it that way.
5. **Carta's causal claim is Carta's.** Row 6 is the report's own interpretation of its data (flattening orgs,
   stronger ICs). Attribute it to Carta, and do not upgrade it into a cause.
