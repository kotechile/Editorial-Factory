**Decision the reader is facing:** Whether the nonrenewal notice in your hand actually ends your policy on the date it prints — and how much runway the carrier owes you first (120 days in Florida, 75 in California, 60 in Washington on a cancellation), because a notice that misses the statutory clock leaves the old policy running at the old premium until the clock is satisfied.
