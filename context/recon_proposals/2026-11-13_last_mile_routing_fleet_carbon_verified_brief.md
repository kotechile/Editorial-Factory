**Gate rulings for the drafter**

- **Do not say the first-year report requires Scope 3, or that Scope 3 begins in 2027.** The mailer only states the first year covers Scope 1 and Scope 2. The 2027 expansion is what this vertical reported on 2026-10-23; it is not re-anchored here.
- **Attribute the 6,000-package count to the company's quarterly disclosure as reported by Supply Chain Dive**, not to the earnings release's own text; the release states the network share and the margin effect, the count comes from the write-up.
- **"5% of outbound volume" is the CEO's target share**, not a measured 2026 share. Write it as the target.
- **Do not compute a carbon saving.** No source in this run ties an insourced package to a grams figure; the piece's claim is about *which line* the tonne lands on, never about how many tonnes.
- **The EPA definition and the GHG Protocol category are definitions, not events.** They carry no date and must be presented as the rules of the ledger, not as news.
