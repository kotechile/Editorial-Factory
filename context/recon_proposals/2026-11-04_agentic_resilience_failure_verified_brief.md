**Gate rulings for the drafter**

1. **The 88% is a goodput figure over the following hour, not a throughput figure at the moment of collapse.** Do not write "goodput fell 88% at the peak" or "the engine lost 88% of its capacity"; write it as the hour after a ten-minute burst, or use the 17-vs-138 pair instead.
2. **The +33% surge recovered; the +100% surge did not.** Any sentence that generalises to "a surge traps an engine" is wrong. The paper's own condition is that the working set crosses roughly twice the cache capacity, and that recovery then depends on whether completions can exceed arrivals at the base load that follows.
3. **"More cache" is a boundary move, not a cure** (row 9). Never write that capacity fixes metastability. The paper's own line: it "moves the cliff" and leaves a finite surge able to cross it.
4. **The 0.95 → 0.80 → 0.3 hit-rate path is two stages** (gradual decline to 0.80, then a sharp drop near the cliff). Do not compress it into "the hit rate fell to 0.3 as the surge hit".
5. **17% is "the outcome of tasks beyond the noise floor", measured on a 50-task probe with three admitted changes**, and it is not a "failure rate". 18 up and 20 down: the average is near zero and the claim is about reach, not direction. Never present it as a regression.
6. **0.000 vs −0.16 is a behavioural similarity score against a 0.98 floor**, not a success-rate difference. Do not convert it into "16% worse".
7. **The 9-of-37 collapse belongs to a diversity gate on a training-set judge**, not to the agent's own verifier, and it is a 0-vs-0.250 range. Cite it as an example of a gate that looks healthy, never as an agent accuracy result.
8. **The llm-d issue is dated 2026-09-22, outside the 30-day window.** It may be cited as what operators are shipping (friction) but never as an in-window anchor; its measurement was made on a different workload (Claude Code sessions) from the paper's (Terminal-Bench).
9. **The Zendesk incident has no published technical mechanism.** Its root cause is one sentence. Use it as a timeline and a caution, attribute it as the vendor's own incident report, and do not assert that the same cache mechanism caused it.
10. **Removed / not usable:** any claim that the paper measured a multi-tenant fleet or a production outage (it measured one SGLang deployment and simulated the rest); any conversion of sessions/h into dollars; any statement that Basin is available to buy.
