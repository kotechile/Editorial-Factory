## Gate rulings for the drafter
1. **It is not amnesty.** § 1592(c)(4) caps the penalty only if the disclosure precedes a formal investigation and the duty is tendered. Say "capped", never "forgiven"; the duties are owed under § 1592(d) in every case (row 5).
2. **The 10-day and 30-day figures are the article's actionability.** Use the oral-confirmation rule (row 6) and the tender-or-denial rule (row 8) as the two dated facts. Do not merge them into "file within 30 days".
3. **Interest is not zero.** For negligence and gross negligence the capped amount is interest at the § 6621 rate from the date of liquidation (row 3). The piece must not imply the exposure disappears.
4. **Provenance.** Rows 1–5 and 10 are statute/regulation text; row 11 is CBP's own estimate of collection burden, not a count of violations saved — do not call it "762 disclosures a year actually filed".
5. **No invented arithmetic.** The brief authorises exactly one derived comparison: 4× duties (gross negligence) against interest-only (disclosed, negligent) — stated as two sourced endpoints, never as a computed dollar saving.
6. **Out of scope:** which duty programs (Section 232, IEEPA) are subject to drawback or penalty carve-outs, and the § 6621 rate itself (quarterly, moves). Leave both out of the body rather than dating a figure the brief did not verify.
