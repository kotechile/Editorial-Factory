What a homeowner can compute is $3,489 × 4.3% ≈ $150 a year — the value of the average, on the state's own average premium — against a carrier-level filing that moves 751,000 homes the other way.
**Hook:** Texas published the best rate news in years and the rule that stops your roof's age from deciding whether you can buy insurance — in the same 24 hours, with the largest carrier raising rates on 751,000 homes in between.
**Tension:** The state regulates the *label* of risk (age, an average), not its *price* (condition, a filed rate).
